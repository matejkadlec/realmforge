## 1. Portals, realmstones and the unlock graph (hub? returning?)

### Takeaway
AoA uses **one portal recipe for every dimension**: a 5-wide × 6-tall frame of 13 Ancient Rock + 4 Carved Runes + a Carved Rune of Power (bottom centre); right-clicking the Rune of Power with a dimension's **non-consumed realmstone** lights the frame with that dimension's coloured portal; a Blank Realmstone clears it. Since 3.2, realmstones come from a **task tree ("Deity Challenges")** shown as a pannable map in the Blank Realmstone GUI; the tree has 6 independent branches off the Overworld (tiers 1–4). There is **no hub** in 3.x (Nowhere, added in 3.5, is a hub for bosses/parkour/utility, not for dimensions); returning is easy because keys are permanent, frames are cheap, and each portal remembers where you came from.

### Cited Findings
- Start of the chain: dig deep for a **Ruined Teleporter Frame** "guarded by a Corrupted Traveller. Help him out, and he'll give you something" (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/getting_started.txt`). Frames generate in the Overworld at a default 1-in-130-chunks roll (`A12/source/utils/ConfigurationUtil.java`, `SubCategoryOverworld.ruinedTeleporterFrameSpawnChance = 130`). Giving the Traveller **any food item** yields a **Worn Book** (`A12/source/common/containers/ContainerCorruptedTraveller.java`, `handleFoodInput`, `instanceof ItemFood` → `WORN_BOOK`). Opening the book drops a Blank Realmstone ("As you open the book, a strange item drops out", `A12/lang/en_us.lang` `message.feedback.wornBook.droppedRealmstone`); since 3.2.2 it keeps giving one whenever you don't hold one (`A12/changelogs/3.2.2.md`).
- Official checklist: make shelter → find ruined frame → get Worn Book + Blank Realmstone → collect Ancient Rock from the ruined frame → find the four Carved Runic Blocks in "dungeons, caves, temples, and villages" → craft the fifth, the Carved Rune of Power → build "a 5x6 frame of Ancient Rock, placing each of the four Carved Runic Blocks somewhere in the frame, and the Carved Rune of Power in the middle at the bottom" → complete Blank Realmstone challenges → use realmstones on the Rune of Power (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/what_to_do_first.txt`). The Worn Book lore states "13 of the blackened stone, 5 carved runes" and the Rune of Power recipe (unpowered runes + ender eye around a blackened stone) (`A12/lang/misc/en_us/worn_book.txt`).
- The four placeable runes are Reality, Travel, Space, Direction (+ Power) (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` `tile.CarvedRuneOf*`). The 1.21 guide's multiblock shows the exact pattern (top row 5 rock; runes in the side columns; Power at bottom centre) (`A21/assets/patchouli_books/aoa_essentia/en_us/entries/the_portal.json`); JEI text: "5x6 portal frame out of Ancient Rock and one of each of the five Carved Rune Blocks … Light with a non-blank Realmstone" (`A21/lang/en_us.json` `description.jei.aoa3.portal_frame_block`).
- Activation logic: holding a `Realmstone` and using the Carved Rune of Power runs a frame test and lights the frame with `realmstone.getPortalBlock()`; an already-lit frame answers "The portal remains lit, but it doesn't seem to require your key again"; using a **Blank** Realmstone on the Rune of Power removes the portal block above it (the rest collapses) (`A12/source/block/functional/misc/CarvedRunicPortalBlock.java`; messages in `A12/lang/en_us.lang` `message.feedback.teleporterFrame.*`). The realmstone is **not consumed** (no stack shrink in that code) and has max stack size 1 (`A12/source/item/misc/Realmstone.java`). Each dimension has its own portal block with its own particle colour (`A12/source/block/functional/portal/PortalBlock.java`, constructor `(dimId, particleColour)`), and each lighting plays a flavour line, e.g. Abyss "The darkness of the Abyss creates a chill around you" (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` lines ~3866–3890). 1.21 guide: "One portal can be used to take you to many worlds" (`A21/…/the_portal.json`).
- Portal blocks only survive while bordered by Ancient Rock / carved runes / portal blocks (`PortalBlock.isCompatibleNeighbour`), so frames must be intact (`A12/source/block/functional/portal/PortalBlock.java`).
- **Return logic:** the player stores a per-dimension "portal return location" (which dimension they came from + coordinates). Walking into a portal of dimension X while outside X takes you to X; walking into it inside X takes you back to the stored origin dimension (or the Overworld if none) (`PortalBlock.onEntityCollision`; `A12/source/utils/player/PlayerDataManager.java` `get/setPortalReturnLocation`). The teleporter searches for an existing portal within `portalSearchRadius`, otherwise builds one near the arrival point, and scales coordinates by the two worlds' movement factors (`A12/source/dimension/AoATeleporter.java` lines ~34–92). Wiki (modern): the AoA Nether realmstone portal "does not apply the 8:1 conversion" ([Dimensions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensions)). Portal-search speed and "portal loop" bugs were recurring fixes (`A12/changelogs/3.1.md` l.131; `3.2.3.md` l.28; `3.2.5.md` l.84 "Re-wrote the portal search & generation algorithms"; `3.2.6.md` l.46 void-world freeze).
- Frame parts can be **bought**: the Overworld **Realmshifter** NPC sells each of the four carved runes for 20 Copper Coins and Ancient Rock at 64 per Silver Coin (or 30 per 10 copper, 3 per copper) (`A12/source/entity/npcs/trader/EntityRealmshifter.java` lines 51–57). Realmshifter was removed in 1.19-3.6.15 (`GH:1.19/3.6.15.md` l.11).
- **3.x realmstone tasks** ("Deity Challenges" GUI, `A12/lang/en_us.lang` lines 1703–1727): Nether – throw a Blank Realmstone out while submerged in lava; Abyss – summon a Wither in the Nether; Deeplands – mine an ore block at bedrock level; Crystevia – use a Deep Crystal on a Case Construct; Ancient Cavern – kill 5 different Crystal Constructs in 30 s; Immortallis – take a hit from a Strength-buffed Construct of Strength; Barathos – kill a Husk with a Blank Realmstone; Iromine – use a Blank Realmstone on a Mineralization Station; Precasia – combine a Blank Realmstone with one of each Overworld sapling; Candyland – eat a Heart Fruit in Precasia; Gardencia – throw a Blank Realmstone into a carrot crop in Precasia, then defeat "your creation" (Primitive Carrotop); L'Borean – let a Gardencian creature soak in Candied Water, then kill it; Creeponia – survive a creeper blast with primed TNT nearby; Vox Ponds – show any Creeponia trader your Blank Realmstone while wearing a Hazmat suit; Mysterium – inflict a Creepird with poison; Runandor – take a Vox Light to Mysterium, place Blank Realmstone + Runic Energy on it, activate with an Active Rune Stone; Lelyetia – take a fall of ≥26 blocks; Haven – kill a Flye with melee high up in the Lelyetian trees (Y>80 since 3.3, `A12/changelogs/3.3.md` l.394); Celeve – kill a Clown with a Lelyetian Greatblade; Lunalus – jump over the void in Haven with a Gravitator; Dustopia – summon Shadowlord while Night Vision is active; Greckon – use Nightmare Flakes on Elusive; Shyrelands – combine Blank Realmstone + Magic Mending Solution + Haunted Flower + Glowstone + Darkly Powder + Bloodstone. Implementation examples: Vox Ponds task swaps the held Blank Realmstone for the Vox Ponds Realmstone when used on an `AoATrader` in Creeponia while environmentally protected; Barathos task fires when a Husk dies from a Blank Realmstone hit; Ancient Cavern task tracks 5 construct classes killed within 600 ticks (`A12/source/item/misc/BlankRealmstone.java`).
- The challenge GUI is a pannable/zoomable picture map (1889×1515 background) with each dimension placed at fixed coordinates, Overworld at the centre (827,695) (`A12/source/client/gui/realmstonegui/GuiRealmstoneChallengeMenu.java`). In 1.21 this is data-driven: one JSON per node with `gui_x`, `gui_y`, `parents` (`A21/assets/realmstonegui/overworld.json`, `precasia.json` → parent overworld, `nether.json` → parent overworld, `nowhere.json` no parent). Wiki: "Arrows pointing from one dimension to another show which dimension is the prerequisite … hovering … shows … realmstone task" ([Blank Realmstone](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Blank_Realmstone)).
- **History of key acquisition:** pre-2.0 portals were pre-built structures recreated with **Portal Blueprints** dropped by (often rare) mobs ([Removed features/Portal Blueprints](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Portal_Blueprints_(pre-2.0))); 2.0 introduced Ancient Rock and the Carved Runes ([2.0](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/2.0)) and an **Ancient Teleporter Blueprint** (8 amethyst + diamond) that spawned the whole frame, removed in Tslat 1.1 "due to its griefing potential" ([Removed features/Ancient Teleporter Blueprint](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Ancient_Teleporter_Blueprint)); early 3.x realmstones were mob drops (e.g. "Moved the Ancient Cavern realmstone drop to Angelicas from Automatons", `A12/changelogs/3.0.1.md` l.29; "Added lunalus realmstone drop to Visulon", `3.0.2.md` l.9) and some were time-gated behind Overworld events ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions), "Why were events removed?"); **3.2** switched to the task system: "A base realmstone can be obtained from corrupted travellers. Completing various tasks with this base realmstone will unlock realmstones for other dimensions" (`A12/changelogs/3.2.md` l.13). Wiki on the old design: "Many realmstones were obtained from the overworld in the past … an attempt by the original developer to make content more accessible … dropped in favor of overhauling the dimensions" ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)).
- **1.21 state:** only Overworld → Precasia, Overworld → Nether, and Nowhere are in the realmstone map (`A21/assets/realmstonegui/*.json`). Precasia's key is now "Unearth a buried realmstone from within a jungle" (brushable suspicious gravel) and Nowhere's is crafted (Shulker Box + Nether Star + Void Scales + Recovery Compass + Redstone Lamp + Blank Realmstone) (`A21/lang/en_us.json` `gui.aoa3.realmstoneMenu.hover.*`; `A21/data/aoa3/recipe/crafting_shapeless_nowhere_realmstone.json`). Portals keyed to undeveloped dimensions refuse with "This dimension is not ready yet, still in development :)" (`A21/lang/en_us.json` `message.aoa3.feedback.portal.tba`; `GH:1.20/3.6.30.md` "Disabled portal activation of undeveloped dimensions with a message").
- **Hub:** none in 3.x. Nowhere (added 3.5) is "an endless void with various floating rooms": a Hub whose portals lead to Parkour, Dungeon (unimplemented), Bosses and Utility sections; blocks can't be placed/broken, no item loss on death, skill XP halved, separate always-full hunger outside boss arenas ([Nowhere](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Nowhere)).

### Inferences
- The realmstone is a **reusable key** and the frame a **universal socket**: the one-structure/many-keys model the Realmforge owner already chose. The main friction points in AoA were (a) building the first frame (rune hunting; mitigated by the Realmshifter), (b) needing a Blank Realmstone to clear before re-keying, and (c) obscure, sometimes buggy tasks (many 3.2.x changelog entries fix individual realmstone tasks, e.g. `A12/changelogs/3.2.2.md` l.54–67).
- Tasks deliberately use **items/mobs from the parent dimension** (Deep Crystal → Crystevia, Precasian Heart Fruit → Candyland, Creeponia trader → Vox Ponds), which is what makes the graph a real dependency tree rather than an arbitrary list.

### Gaps
- `AoAPortalFrame.testFrameForActivation` (exact frame validation rules, e.g. orientation tolerance) was not read line by line.
- How common each Carved Rune is in 3.x loot tables could not be checked (loot JSONs are not in the view-only repo). 1.15.2 added "the 4 carved runes for portals to the overworld loot table" (`GH:1.15.2/3.4.md` l.81).

## 2. Dimension tiers, intended order and difficulty scaling

### Takeaway
AoA 3.x had **no single intended order**: 22 AoA dimensions sat on 6 linear branches, and a dimension's **tier = 1 + the highest tier you must visit to reach it** (Overworld = T0). 3.2 ("The Loot Update") made tiers official and rescaled mob stats, weapons and rewards to them. In code, median mob HP climbs ~30 (T0) → ~55–77 (T1) → ~70–95 (T2) → ~115–127 (T3) → ~165 (T4), and the Hunter skill ladder (level 1→97) acts as a second, skill-based ordering of the same content.

### Cited Findings
- "Dimensional tiers represent how far into a path a dimension is … [3.2] made them official and balanced weapon stats, rewards, and dimension difficulty around tiers … The Overworld is Tier 0. A dimension's tier is one more than that of the highest tiered dimension that must be visited to reach it … As dimensions are on independent linear paths, tiers are very straightforward" ([Dimensional Tiers](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensional_Tiers)). "Weapons are tiered based on the highest tier dimension that must be visited to make them" (same page).
- 3.x tier table (wiki revision of July 2020): T1 Creeponia, Nether, Lelyetia, Deeplands, Barathos, Precasia; T2 Vox Ponds, Mysterium, Abyss, Celeve, Haven, Crystevia, Iromine, Gardencia, Candyland; T3 Runandor, Greckon, Dustopia, Lunalus, Immortallis, Ancient Cavern, L'Borean; T4 Shyrelands; the End counted as T2 ([Dimensional Tiers?oldid=92772](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensional_Tiers?oldid=92772)). By 2021 Immortallis/Ancient Cavern were gone and "Nowhere is considered untiered" ([?oldid=107304](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensional_Tiers?oldid=107304)).
- Order is free within the tree: "the player must access some dimensions to access other dimensions. Otherwise, the player is free to visit the dimensions in any order" ([Dimensions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensions)).
- 3.2: "All mobs have had their stats adjusted to scale properly with dimensional progress" and "Numerous mobs now drop crafting components" (`A12/changelogs/3.2.md`).
- **Derived mob stats per dimension** (each mob class's `getBaseMaxHealth()` and melee/projectile damage in `A12/source/entity/mobs/<dimension>/*.java`; median over the dimension's mob classes, HP ≤2 placeholders excluded): Overworld 66 mobs, HP median 30 (10–75), dmg 4; Creeponia 55, dmg 4; Lelyetia 60, dmg 7; Nether 65, dmg 6.75; Crystevia 69, dmg 8; Barathos 70 (one 200-HP outlier), dmg 7.5; Deeplands 70, dmg 7; Precasia 76.5, dmg 8.5; Mysterium 77, dmg 8.75; Celeve 85; Haven 86; Candyland 87; Iromine 89, dmg 10.5; Gardencia 92.5; Abyss 95; Vox Ponds 95, dmg 11.75; Runandor 115, dmg 13; Lunalus 120, dmg 14.75; L'Borean 122, dmg 14; Greckon 124, dmg 13.5; Dustopia 127, dmg 13.75; Shyrelands 164.5 (140–180), dmg 16 (Shyre Knight: 140 HP, 18 melee, 19 armour). Overworld event mobs sit at 32–80 HP (Big Day giants 55–95). (Derived via `entity_stats.py` + `summarize_stats.py`.) For scale, the player has 20 HP and Innervation adds at most +20 (see §3).
- Example tool-material damage bonuses (sword attack damage parameter) by source, with the dev's own dimension comments: Limonite 5.0 (Overworld), Rosite 6.5, Jade 7.0, Amethyst 7.5, Sapphire 10.0, Emberstone 11.0, Baron 12.0 (Barathos), Bloodfury 13.0 / Bloodstone 14.0 (Abyss), Shroomus 15.0, Rosidian 15.5, Shadow 17.0, Candlefire 17.0 (Nether upgrade kit), Runic 17.5, Harvester 17.5 (`A12/source/common/registration/MaterialsRegister.java` lines 11–46; dimension/source comments in `WeaponRegister.java`, e.g. l.478–485 "// Abyss | Crafting", "// UPGRADE KIT Nether").
- **Hunter ladder (3.3.6 defaults)** — creatures you literally cannot damage below the level (71 entries; `A12/source/utils/ConfigurationUtil.java` l.1624 `hunterEntities`; dimension from entity folders): Overworld/event mobs lvl 1–12 (giants 9, Amphibiyte 12); Nether Skeletal Cowman 13, Nethengeic Beast 24; Precasia Kaiyu 17, Diocus 27, Spinoledon 28, Iosaur 29; Lelyetia Zhinx 19, Exohead 23, Paravite 28, Rawbone 30; Mysterium Mushroom Spider 29, Undead Troll 32, Nightmare Spider/Banshee 34, Phantom 44; Iromine Enforcer 31; Abyss Jawe 33, Flesh Eater 40; Haven Dawnlight 35; Vox Ponds Nightwing 39; Celeve Jumbo 42; Candyland Cane Bug 47, Gingerbird 50, Gingerbread Man 53; Gardencia Pod Plant 56, Vine Wizard 59; Runandor Spectral Wizard 64, Runic Guardian 66; L'Borean Mermage 67, Amphibior 69; Greckon Faceless Floater 71; Lunalus Zorp 75 … Inmate X/Y 81; Dustopia Merkyre 83, Crusilisk 84, Arkzyne 86; Shyrelands Shyre Troll 90, Lightwalker 94, Luxocron 97. ### Inferences
- The tiering is **graph-derived, not level-derived**: difficulty is attached to the dimension, and the player's power comes from gear of that tier. HP roughly ×1.4–1.6 per tier step after T1 while melee damage roughly doubles from T1 to T4 — a usable template for chapter-based stat bands.
- Hunter gating created a **second, skill-based order** that sometimes disagrees with tiers (e.g. Mysterium T2 hosts lvl 29–44 Hunter mobs while Candyland T2 hosts 47–53); players had to grind a skill to fight parts of a dimension they could already enter.

### Gaps
- No official "recommended order" list for 3.x beyond the tier table was found; the wiki explicitly says order is free within the tree.
- Boss HP does not track tiers cleanly (e.g. T1 Tyrosaur 4,000 HP vs T3 Bane 1,750 HP, `A12/source/entity/boss/*`), but bosses also differ in armour/mechanics; I did not model effective difficulty.

## 3. Skills and resources

### Takeaway
3.x had **15 skill slots** (Alchemy and Engineering never implemented; Augury "temporarily deactivated") — 12 working skills, each levelled 1–100 by its own activity with an exponential curve (50 × 1.1^level XP per level) where each action yields a fixed fraction of the next level, so every level needs a **fixed number of actions per band** (e.g. Butchery ≈3,600 bloodlust pickups to 100, Anima ≈9,600, Expedition ≈44,000 movement procs). Four resources (Rage, Energy, Creation, Soul; cap 200) powered weapons, minions and tablets. In 1.16.5-3.6 all of it was wiped "for a clean slate": 10 new data-driven skills with toggleable level abilities and "cycles", and 3 resources (Energy, Rage, Spirit); in 1.21, 6 of 10 skills are trainable.

### Cited Findings
- Skill enum: Alchemy, Anima, Augury, Butchery, Creation, Engineering, Expedition, Extraction, Foraging, Hauling, Hunter, Infusion, Innervation, Logging, Runation; resources: Creation, Energy, Rage, Soul; deities: Erebon, Luxon, Pluton, Selyan (`A12/source/library/Enums.java` l.18–236). "Alchemy is not yet implemented", "Engineering is not yet implemented", "Augury has been temporarily deactivated" (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/alchemy.txt`, `engineering.txt`, `augury.txt`).
- How each 3.x skill levels and what it gives (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/*.txt`): **Anima** – random Anima stones from plants/leaves/crops, Fragmented Anima Stones; unlocks soul **tablets** (placeable AoE effects powered by Soul). **Butchery** – red "bloodlust" orbs while meleeing; faster Battle Rage recharge and bigger rage hits. **Creation** – craft minion slabs and sacrifice them at a Creation Forge; higher levels summon stronger minions. **Expedition** – passive XP while moving; more "leg power", less fall damage, and a chosen random buff (speed / satiation / deep breath). **Extraction** – Extraction Device fed with lava source blocks, empty with a bowl, clear obsidian; better loot with level. **Foraging** – loot while mining ordinary stone. **Logging** – loot while chopping. **Hauling** – better fish and treasure while fishing. **Hunter** – see §2 (gates "hunter creatures"). **Infusion** – harvest Pixons with a stone/diamond bowl for infusion/power stones, insert stones at the Infusion Table; unlocks enchant recipes and higher pixons. **Innervation** – heart stones from kills; permanent max-health increase. **Runation** – smelt runium, craft blank runes, charge them at dimension rune shrines (staff ammo); higher levels unlock more rune types and free duplicates.
- XP curve: XP to next level = 50 × 1.1^level up to 100; above 100: (min(level,999)−10)^2.5/100 + 630,000; level 1000 caps (`A12/source/utils/player/PlayerUtil.java` `getXpRequiredForNextLevel`). Effects use `min(100, level)`; display shows up to 1000 (`PlayerDataManager.getLevel/getLevelForDisplay`). Derived: lvl 10→11 needs ~130 XP, lvl 50→51 ~5,870, lvl 99→100 ~626,000; total 1→100 ≈ 6.89 M.
- XP per action = (XP for next level) ÷ denominator(level band) — e.g. heart stones, bloodlust, anima stones, extraction, expedition (`A12/source/entity/misc/EntityHeartStone.java` l.101; `A12/source/entity/misc/EntityBloodlust.java` l.66; `A12/source/block/functional/utility/ExtractionDevice.java` l.140; `A12/source/utils/skills/ExpeditionUtil.java` l.57). Denominator ladders (actions per level): Butchery 4 → 7 → 11 → 20 → 35 → 45 → 60 → 80 → 110; Innervation 4 → … → 150; Anima 4 → 8 → 30 → 60 → 85 → 112 → 180 → 220 → 280; Extraction 5 → … → 300; Expedition 22 → … → 1,300 (`A12/source/utils/skills/*Util.java`). Derived totals to level 100: Butchery ≈3,600 orbs, Innervation ≈4,600 heart stones, Anima ≈9,600 stones, Extraction ≈12,400 successes (bands to 98), Expedition ≈44,000 procs.
- Innervation's health bonus: floor(min(100,lvl)/5) → +20 HP at 100 (`A12/source/utils/skills/InnervationUtil.java` l.42–43).
- Skill gates elsewhere: Ancient Cavern bosses need level 70 Hunter/Runation/Foraging/Infusion (§4); Infusion level gates Pixon harvesting and each enchant recipe (`A12/source/crafting/recipes/InfusionTableRecipe.java`; [Removed features/Infusion](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Infusion): Glistening Pixon lvl 1 … Blooming lvl 81; Control I–IV at 17/34/51/72; Brace 61; Archmage I–III 70/80/89). Skill armour (e.g. Hunter armour) is worn at level 100 ([Removed features/Hunter (skill)](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Hunter_(skill))) and sold by per-skill **Skill Masters** for Lunaver coins (`A12/source/entity/npcs/skillmaster/*Master.java`, trades priced in `LUNAVER_COIN`). Skill Crystals grant XP "to Your Lowest Level Skill" (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` `items.description.skillCrystal.desc.1`).
- Resources (3.x): all cap at 200; per-tick regen Energy +0.32 (≈31 s to full, paused after use), Creation +0.033 (≈5 min), Soul +0.01 (≈17 min), Rage at a Butchery-dependent rate (`A12/source/utils/player/PlayerDataManager.java` l.752–762). ### Inferences
- 3.x skills were **parallel grinds**, each with its own unique activity and reward, which gave variety but also 12 separate 1–100 tracks (several thousand actions each) layered on top of dimension progression; the 3.6 rewrite's explicit goal ("much more palatable") implies the old rates were felt as grind.
- Several 3.x skills worked as **hard gates** (Hunter, Infusion level per pixon/enchant, AC level-70 shrines) rather than soft bonuses; the rewrite moved to soft, toggleable ability unlocks — a design shift toward "profession perks" rather than "permission to play".
- Resources were tightly coupled to weapon classes (Energy→blasters, Soul→tablets, Creation→minions, Rage→crits) — elegant per-class economies, but each added HUD and balancing surface; the rewrite cut to three.

### Gaps
- No primary player-survey/Reddit data on which skills felt grindy (Reddit/CurseForge comments were not reachable: HTTP 403 / login wall). Evidence of grind is indirect (code numbers, rewrite changelog wording, developer quotes).
- Exact 3.x Runation/Creation/Hauling level tables were not extracted.

## 4. Bosses, summoning and the boss-focused dimensions

### Takeaway
3.3.x had **35 boss fights**, almost all optional, each tied to a dimension and summoned by a dimension item (dropped on the ground, used on a structure-bound altar, or a special action); only two boss summons are unlock tasks (the vanilla Wither for the Abyss realmstone, Shadowlord for Dustopia's). Two "structure dimensions" were boss/dungeon hubs: **Ancient Cavern** (4 deity champions gated by skill 70 + full tribute) and **Immortallis** (a 9-room progression dungeon with 4 "Immortals"). The rewrite moved **all boss fights into Nowhere's indestructible random arenas**, with non-consumed totems and harder "elite" versions summoned with a gold trophy.

### Inferences
- AoA's 3.x bosses were **summon-on-demand world bosses** (repeatable with the item), not dungeon ends; few were placed in dungeons. The modern design (instanced arena + reusable totem + elite tier) is effectively an instanced "boss dungeon" without trash.
- The Ancient Cavern/Immortallis pattern — a dedicated "dungeon realm" whose doors open via meta-currencies (tribute, progress coins) — failed partly because those currencies were themselves arbitrary.

### Gaps
- 3.x per-boss loot tables (exact drop chances) are not in the view-only repo; only the generic 2020 wiki description was available.

## 5. Economy: coins, tokens, traders, tribute and deities

### Takeaway
3.x used a **universal 4-step coin ladder** (Copper → Silver → Gold → Lunaver, 20:1 via bankers) plus **21 per-dimension token currencies** dropped by that dimension's mobs and spent at a clone "Lottoman" in each dimension (loot boxes) or the Undead Herald (banners). Traders were few, dimension-flavoured and mostly sold that dimension's armour/weapons/ammo. **Tribute** was a daily Overworld meta-game with four gods. In 3.5–3.6 tokens and tribute were removed in favour of coins and no deity gameplay.

### Cited Findings
- Coin exchange: bankers trade 20 lower coins for 1 higher (and back) across Copper/Silver/Gold/Lunaver (`A12/source/common/containers/ContainerBankerTrade.java`, slot logic `shrink(… 20 …)`, `getCoinForSlot`); derived: 1 Lunaver = 20 Gold = 400 Silver = 8,000 Copper. Bankers exist in Creeponia, Lelyetia, Dustopia, Shyrelands, Lunalus (`A12/source/entity/npcs/banker/`). Special coins: Circus Coin (Celeve Toy Merchant), Silvro Coin (Silverfoot), Progress Coins (Immortallis) (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` `item.*Coin.name`; trader parses).
- Tokens: "Each dimension had its own tokens … Tokens were part of World Tables … most AoA mobs would drop tokens"; Lottoman prices: Lotto Totem 28, Weapons Case 15, Rune Box 10, Treasure Box 21 tokens; removed in 3.5 "in favor of using coins as a universal currency" ([Removed features/Tokens](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Tokens); [GH:1.16.5/3.5-Beta-5.md](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/blob/1.16.5/changelogs/3.5-Beta-5.md) "Removed all dimensional Tokens … Changed all lottomen to use coins"). 22 Lottoman classes, each selling the same four items for its dimension's tokens (`A12/source/entity/npcs/lottoman/*.java`). Undead Herald sells a dimension banner for 10 of that dimension's tokens (`A12/source/entity/npcs/trader/EntityUndeadHerald.java` l.67) and has a spawn entry in all 20 natural AoA dimensions plus Overworld and Nether (22 entries, `A12/source/common/registration/EntitySpawnRegister.java`). 3.2 world tables: every dimension's mobs share a "world" loot table (seeds, coins, tokens, materials) with access to a "rare loot" table (`A12/changelogs/3.2.md`).
- 3.x traders (derived from `A12/source/entity/npcs/trader/*.java` trade lists): Overworld wanderers – Assassin (thrown weapons, ammo for copper), Metalloid (ingots/gems ↔ copper), Naturalist (buys crops for copper), Realmshifter (portal parts), Troll Trader (coral items), Undead Herald; Crystevia – Crystal Trader (crystals → Crystevia tokens, druses, crystal weapons); Creeponia – Explosives Expert (grenades, Omni armour); Mysterium – Gorb Arms Dealer, Gorb Engineer (Phantasm armour, Ultraflame); Lelyetia – Lelyetian Trader (Gauge Rifle, glass); Iromine – Professor (Mecha gear, Gold Spring boss item, Super Cannon); Vox Ponds – Store Keeper; Celeve – Toy Merchant (Circus Coins → Gravitator, Toy Gyrocopter boss item, Smiley Upgrade Kit); Dustopia – Primordial Merchant/Spellbinder/Wizard (Primordial armour, runes, Energistic tools); Lunalus – Zal Grocer/Herbalist/Spellbinder/Vendor; Shyrelands – Shyre Archer (Sunshine Bow, Ancient Ring boss item); Immortallis – Dungeon Keeper, Token Collector. Ambient NPCs: Gorb/Zal citizens, Primordial Guide, Portal Master (`A12/source/entity/npcs/ambient/`).
- **Tribute (3.x code):** four bars, 0–200 each (`PlayerDataManager.addTribute` clamps 0–200). Filled during Overworld daytime: Erebon +8 per mob killed (and Luxon −8) (`A12/source/event/PlayerEvents.java` l.200–201); Selyan +2 per fully-grown crop (l.266); Pluton +(11 − level/10) per Foraging/Logging loot proc (l.294, l.328), +4 per Extraction success (`ExtractionDevice.java` l.144); Luxon +4 per Pixon harvest roll (`item/tool/misc/InfusionBowl.java` l.84) and +4×size at the Ascension Shrine (`AscensionShrine.java` l.48). At nightfall (tick 12500) each full bar announces a favour and gives a night-long buff in the Overworld: Luxon Invisibility, Erebon Strength I, Pluton Luck II, Selyan Speed I; all four ≥100 → "Krasaun" message + Night Vision 5 min; all four = 200 → a Holy Sword; bars reset at sunrise and on death (`A12/source/event/dimension/OverworldEvents.java` l.56–143; `PlayerDataManager.doTributeBuffs` l.765–778). Wiki adds: 25 kills / 100 crops / 50 pixon rolls fill a bar; sleeping and changing dimension reset progress; states the Krasaun threshold as 75% ([Removed features/Tribute](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Tribute)) — **conflicts with the 3.3.6 code (≥100 of 200 = 50%)**.
- Removal reasons: tribute was "relegated to the Overworld"; "hard to balance"; "actions required to earn points were often arbitrary … some gods were easier to please than others"; "another avenue to obtain potion effects … redundant"; thematic elements "barebones and only served to generate a false idea that there was more to it"; the four gods "are unlikely to return", the lore now uses a single god, Krasaun ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)).
- 1.21: coins Copper/Silver/Gold/Lunaver (+ legacy Circus Coin) and **Archaic Tokens** (Nowhere boss rewards); NPC entity types reduced to Corrupted Traveller, Lottoman, Skill Master, Undead Herald (`A21/lang/en_us.json` `item.aoa3.*coin*`, `entity.aoa3.*`; [Bosses](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses)).

### Inferences
- Per-dimension tokens + identical Lottomen made every dimension's economy a **copy of the same loop** (kill → tokens → random box), which adds grind but no local identity; AoA itself replaced it with one currency.
- Trader stock was the main **non-combat reason to visit a dimension again** (dimension armour, ammo, boss items), but with 1–3 traders per dimension that reason was thin.

### Gaps
- Exact Skill Master prices and level requirements in 3.3.6 were not parsed; 1.21 trade tables were not examined.

## 6. Gear: armour, weapon classes, upgrade paths, infusion and consumables

### Takeaway
AoA 3.3.6 registered **412 weapons in 13 classes** and ~261 armour pieces (~65 sets incl. 15 skill sets); each dimension added 5–23 weapons with unique effects. Progression came from **dimension materials + crafting** (~170), **Upgrade Kits** at a Divine Station (~70; the station itself only generated in tier-4 Shyrelands), boss/trader/token sources, and from 3.2 the **Frame Bench + Infusion Table** (infuse frames into gear; imbue AoA enchants with Pixon-derived power stones). Consumables were mostly short bursts (2–10 s food buffs), staff runes, ammo, loot boxes and soul tablets; there were no potions (Alchemy unimplemented).

### Cited Findings
- Weapon counts by class (registered `Base*` fields): guns 78, staves 57, blasters 56, cannons 50, swords 36, bows 33, greatblades 29, snipers 27, shotguns 17, archerguns 9, vulcanes 8, thrown 7, mauls 5 = 412; armour fields 261 (`A12/source/common/registration/WeaponRegister.java`, `ArmourRegister.java`). - Dev comments on 405 weapons give source/dimension: ~170 "Crafting", ~70 "UPGRADE KIT", the rest boss drops (Primordial, Gyro, Vinocorne, Baroness, Bane, Crystocore…), traders, token traders, mob drops; per dimension Overworld 30, Iromine 23, Gardencia 17, Celeve 17, Barathos/Haven/Crystevia 16, Shyrelands 15, Abyss 14, Nether/Precasia/Lunalus 12 … Creeponia 5 (derived from `WeaponRegister.java` comments).

Vulcanes punish an attacker whose soul was "linked" when you were hit (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/screen_elements.txt`).
- Upgrade kits (18): Abyssal, Ancient, Apoco, Clown, Darkly, Floro, Golden, Haunted, Lelyetian, Light, Lunar, Nether, Precasian, Predator, Rocky, Runic, Seaside, Smiley (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` `item.*UpgradeKit.name`); Divine Station: weapon + kit → upgraded weapon; in 3.x "Divine stations can be found on Divine Platforms, which spawn in The Shyrelands" ([Divine Station?oldid=97998](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Station?oldid=97998); `A12/source/dimension/shyrelands/ChunkGenShyrelands.java` l.436).
- 3.2: "A frame bench … converts scrap metal into frame items … base templates for several weapon and armour item types"; "Infusion is now split into two parts: Infusing, and Imbuing" (`A12/changelogs/3.2.md` l.14–15). Infusing = frame + materials → new item; Imbuing = item + enhancers + power stones → enchant (`A12/lang/guides/en_us/infusion.txt`). AoA enchantments: Archmage (−1 rune cost/level), Brace (off-hand firing), Control (−recoil), Crush (maul knockback), Form (shotgun spread), Curse of Greed, Intervention (keep on death, may break), Recharge (blaster energy), Sever (greatblade damage), Shell (gun damage) (`A12/lang/en_us.lang` `enchantment.aoa3.*`). Infusion stones become a Power Stone with 1/200 chance (1/35 for Ambient) when inserted ([Removed features/Infusion](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Infusion)).
- Consumables: foods with short effects, e.g. Fungal Tea Regeneration IV for 75 ticks (3.75 s) + Resistance I 60 ticks; Natural Tea Resistance II 150 ticks (7.5 s); Magic Marang Night Vision + Glowing 200 ticks (10 s); Luna Globe Invisibility 120 ticks; Goldicap Petals Speed II + Jump II 40 ticks (`A12/source/item/food/*.java` `PotionEffect` calls). - 1.21: same weapon classes plus crossbows; blasters now consume **Spirit**, vulcanes **Rage** ("the next thing that attacks you will be marked … use the vulcane"), staves consume runes, cannons disable shields and add knockback (`A21/assets/patchouli_books/aoa_essentia/en_us/entries/blasters.json`, `vulcanes.json`, `staves.json`, `cannons.json`, `crossbows.json`). - Developer view on overworld tools: balancing many overworld tool materials "without overtaking nether equipment didn't really work, causing redundancy with multiple tools at or next to Diamond level" ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)).

### Inferences
- Gear was AoA's **largest content investment** (412 weapons vs ~2–9 structure types per dimension, §7); the satisfying part was variety and dimension-themed effects, the weak parts were redundancy, ammo micromanagement (bullets, cannonballs, spreadshot, holly arrows, 15 rune types, energy) and late-gated upgrade kits (station only in T4 Shyrelands).
- Pixons are a neat example of **ambient, harvestable creatures** that feed a crafting system — one of the few non-hostile "life" elements in 3.x dimensions.

### Gaps
- Exact infusion/upgrade recipes (JSON) are not in the view-only repo; no quantitative weapon DPS-by-tier table was built.

## 7. World content density and the "empty dimensions" problem

### Takeaway
The perception of empty AoA dimensions is well supported by the 3.3.6 data: **every AoA dimension was a single biome**, had **2–9 named structure types** generated at default rolls of 1-in-83 to 1-in-1700 per chunk (≈1 point of interest per 26–212 chunks, i.e. one every ~80–230 blocks), **0–2 passive animal species** (15 of 22 dimensions (13 of the 20 open-world ones) had none), 1–3 NPC spawn types, and 4–11 hostile spawn entries. Structures were hand-coded block-by-block in Java (≈258k lines), which made them costly, few and static. Meanwhile the Overworld had ~95 AoA mobs. The official explanation for the overhaul cites bloat, low-quality models/AI, single-purpose content and the push "away from the Overworld"; the 1.21 rewrite of Precasia (13 biomes, ~27–36 placed features each, passive + hostile fauna) shows the intended fix.

Worldgen structures in 1.21: attercopus nest, baron resource pit, nethengeic pit, Precasian lotto hovel, ruined teleporter frame + Nowhere arenas/lobbies/parkour (`A21/data/aoa3/worldgen/structure/`).
- Community criticism found only indirectly: the wiki acknowledges complaints that Nowhere "as a single room, was not a good replacement" and that the gods' content was "barebones" ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)). YouTube has community videos such as "Advent of Ascension - Ranking Every Dimension!" and "Advent of Ascension: Dimension Analysis! - Part 1" (search results page https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=advent+of+ascension+review), but their content could not be read.

### Inferences
- "Emptiness" in AoA is structural, not accidental: single-biome infinite worlds + sparse, code-built POIs + hostile-only ecology + identical service structures (lotto, rune shrine) per dimension. The content budget went into weapons, hostile mobs and bosses rather than into ambient life and mid-sized landmarks.
- The rewrite's direction — multi-biome realms built from data-driven features/structures with passive and hostile fauna — is exactly the opposite lever, and it shows why it took years: each realm becomes a full worldgen project.

### Gaps
- No access to r/feedthebeast or CurseForge comment threads (HTTP 403/login wall); WebFetch was rate-limited during this session. Direct player quotes about "big empty dimensions", grind and FPS were therefore **not** collected; the claim rests on code metrics and the official wiki's acknowledgements.
- I did not measure dimension terrain scale (e.g. average distance between landmarks in actual worlds); densities are config-roll estimates.

## 8. Version history: what existed when, and what the rewrite cut

### Takeaway
Nevermine/AoA was created by Xolova in 2014 (1.7.2/1.7.10, versions 1.0–2.4), taken over by Tslat in 2018, fully rewritten as 3.0 for 1.12.2 (Feb 2019) and expanded through 3.3.6 (Sep 2020) — the content peak (22 AoA dimensions, ~35 bosses, 12 working skills, tribute). 1.15.2 (3.4) was a port; 1.16.5 (3.5–3.6.11) removed Ancient Cavern/Immortallis/tokens/many mobs and reworked skills/resources/tribute. From 1.18.2 on, old models could not render, so dimensions were left out until overhauled; by 1.21.1 only Overworld and Nether are "complete" and Precasia and Nowhere "playable but incomplete". Development was formally paused in December 2025.

### Inferences
- AoA's history is a cautionary tale about **breadth outrunning depth and tooling**: 22 single-biome dimensions with hand-coded structures and ~300 old-model mobs could not survive engine changes, so the rewrite restarted almost from zero.
- What survived every rewrite is the **portal/realmstone skeleton**, the **tier rule**, coins, the Lottoman, the Corrupted Traveller start, and the weapon-class taxonomy — the parts that are systems rather than bespoke content.

### Gaps
- Exact list of 1.7.10-era dimensions per version (e.g. when each of the 22 was added) was not compiled; not needed for the cross-system view.

## Relevance to Realmforge

### Adopt
- **One frame, many reusable keys, coloured portals** (owner decision on realm portals): copy AoA's rules, not its names — keys are never consumed (AoA realmstones stack to 1 and survive use), one frame can be re-keyed, each realm has its own portal colour/particles and an activation line of flavour text (`CarvedRunicPortalBlock`, `PortalBlock`). Improve on AoA by letting the **selector block** switch destination directly (AoA needed a Blank Realmstone to clear first).
- **Per-player return memory + 1:1 coordinates + auto-built return portal near arrival**: a portal inside realm X leads back to the realm you entered X from; the destination portal is found within a search radius or built (AoA `AoATeleporter`, `PlayerDataManager.portalReturnLocation`). Fits the owner's "two realms at once" and "hates walking" rules; budget for the portal-search edge cases AoA kept fixing (void worlds, ceilings, portal loops).
- **Data-driven progression map**: AoA 1.21 stores the unlock screen as one JSON per realm (`gui_x`, `gui_y`, `parents`) with hover text for the unlock condition (`A21/assets/realmstonegui/`). Use the same shape for Realmforge's realm map; show which realms are optional side branches.
- **Explicit stat bands per tier/chapter**: AoA's median mob HP ~30 → ~55–77 → ~70–95 → ~115–127 → ~165 and damage 4 → ~7 → ~9–12 → ~13–15 → 16 across T0–T4 is a ready reference for chapter bands (levels 1–10, 10–20, …), with the player HP ceiling in mind (AoA: 20 + max 20 from Innervation).
- **Boss fights in protected arenas** (already in Realmforge architecture: ground bosses, reset if pulled out): AoA's evidence for why — sandbox cheese, mobs interfering, bosses lost in holes/void, drops destroyed — plus useful details: 5 s warning before spawn, exit item (Return Crystal), void-fall returns boss to arena, loot goes straight to inventory and is split in groups, **+25% boss HP per extra player** for the later party system ([Bosses](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses)).
- **Wave-survival archetype**: AoA's Skeletal Army (≈39–40 escalating waves in a Precasia arena, finale Skeletron) is a direct precedent for the owner's wave-survival instance type.
- **Elite repeat of a cleared boss** (AoA: gold trophy → elite version, totem not consumed) as optional replay content without new assets.
- **Mob status icons**: AoA's overhead icon language (immune to fire/melee/ranged/magic/explosions/potions, "special mechanics" marker, level colour) is a compact readability tool for a WoW-like combat layer.

### Adapt
- **Unlock tasks that use the previous realm's stuff**: AoA's best tasks require an item or mob from the parent realm (Deep Crystal → Crystevia; Precasian Heart Fruit → Candyland; showing the key to a Creeponia trader → Vox Ponds). For Realmforge: craft the next realm item from the current realm's ore/gem plus the realm boss drop, and announce it via quest text — not hidden puzzles (AoA's obscure tasks produced many bug reports and needed a wiki).
- **Branching tree → mostly linear with paired realms**: AoA's 6 independent T1 branches made order free but diluted direction; Realmforge's chapter model (one or two realms per 10-level band, e.g. desert + tundra for 10–20) is a narrowed version of the same tier rule ("tier = 1 + highest prerequisite").
- **Friendly NPC traders inside realms**: AoA's useful traders sold boss-summon items, portal parts and realm gear (Realmshifter, Toy Merchant, Professor, Dungeon Keeper). For the M1 mines selling dungeon keys, keep one **universal currency** (AoA itself replaced 21 per-dimension tokens with coins in 3.5) and use realm materials as secondary costs.
- **Pixons → ambient harvestable "wisps"**: neutral, glowing creatures per realm that a profession harvests for enchanting/imbuing materials (AoA tiers them by realm) add life and a reason to roam — good for "every realm feels full".
- **Soul tablets → placeable auras**: AoA's placeable AoE totems (cost on placement + drain per tick, radius 7–25, unlocked by skill level) map onto the owner's toggle auras; AoA's numbers show how quickly a strong aura should drain (Strength tablet ≈25–30 s of a full pool).
- **Daily "favour" mechanic** only if it is realm-bound and long-lasting: AoA's tribute (fill a bar by daytime activity → night buff) failed because it was Overworld-only, arbitrary and duplicated potions. If Realmforge wants something similar, align it with the owner's food/potion rule (weak but long, e.g. +5% for an hour) instead of AoA's 2–10 s food bursts.
- **Skill ability lists from data**: AoA 1.21's per-skill JSON with abilities at level thresholds, toggleable per player (`A21/data/aoa3/player/skills/*.json`) fits Realmforge's passive/profession skills; drop "cycles" unless an endless layer is wanted.
- **Upgrade kits** as a gear path: weapon + realm kit → upgraded weapon is a cheap way to keep early weapons relevant; don't gate the upgrade station behind the last realm as AoA did (Divine Station only in Shyrelands).

### Avoid
- **Single-biome realms with sparse, code-built landmarks and no fauna** — the root of AoA's "empty dimension" feel: 1 biome each, 2–9 structure types at 1/100–1/1700 per chunk, 15 of 22 dimensions (13 of the 20 open-world ones) without a single passive animal. Each Realmforge realm should be one biome *style* with several biomes/sub-biomes, many small structures and landmarks, neutral/passive fauna and vegetation (the AoA 1.21 Precasia rebuild — 13 biomes, ~30 features each — is the model AoA itself moved to).
- **Copy-paste service template per realm** (same lottery NPC + rune shrine + boss altar everywhere): give each realm its own NPC roles and its own reason to return.
- **Hand-coded structures** (AoA: ~258k lines of `addBlock` calls; Kaiyu Temple 3,510 lines): already covered by ADR 0012 (NBT rooms + jigsaw/processors); AoA shows the cost of the alternative.
- **Content breadth over depth**: 412 weapons, 13 ammo-hungry weapon classes and ~95 Overworld mobs vs. thin realms; Realmforge already lets spells carry class identity — keep weapon/ammo types few and the Overworld light (levels 1–5 only).
- **Hard gates on fighting** (AoA Hunter: mobs immune until a skill level; AC shrines at skill 70 + full tribute) and **long parallel skill grinds** (several thousand actions per skill to 100, 12 skills) — the owner dislikes grind (Craft to Exile); use character level for combat gating and keep professions short.
- **Time-gated or random-event keys** (pre-3.2 realmstones from Overworld events/rare drops) and meta-currencies that open dungeons (tribute, progress coins) — both were removed by AoA as frustrating or arbitrary.
- **Limited-visibility horror realms**: AoA's darkness-themed Dustopia even required Night Vision in its unlock task; consistent with the owner's "no horror dimension with limited visibility".
- **Chasing MC versions with bespoke assets** (AoA lost almost all dimensions at 1.18.2+ and paused in Dec 2025): already in Realmforge research notes — keep content in data and pin the version.
- **AoA names and assets**: don't reuse terms like Realmstone, Carved Rune, Ancient Rock, Nowhere, Lottoman, Pixon, tribute deity names; AoA's licence also claims the official wiki content.
