## 1. Which professions exist, how they level, what they unlock, and how they feed each other (stations and recipes)

### Takeaway
CTE2 runs 4 gathering professions (Mining, Farming, Fishing, and Bounty Hunting — the old Husbandry/"Animal Breeding" slot repurposed), 4 crafting professions (Cooking, Alchemy, Gear Crafting, Infusing) and Salvaging. All level 1–100 on the character XP curve, cannot exceed the character's combat level, and switch material tier every 20 levels (tiers start at levels 1/20/40/60/80/100, tied to dimension progress). Crafting is a deterministic "tier material × rarity Stone × flavour ingredient × coin" formula spread over 810 data files; Salvaging turns combat loot into the rarity Stones every recipe needs.

"Due to new Stardew Fishing minigame, Fishing Profession experience has doubled" (0.9.0) — `ACL:2121`.
- XP per gathering action = `(baseXP × (1 + 0.2 × (profLvl − 1)))^1.1` × profession-XP stat multiplier × low-tier penalty — `SRC/.../profession/ExpSources.java` (`giveExp`), `LevelUtils.scaleExpReward`. Computed: a 30-XP crop gives 42 XP at level 1, 130 at 10, 236 at 20, 461 at 40, 1,187 at 100 → ~9 crops for 1→2, ~27 at level 10, ~48 at level 19; a 15-XP iron ore → ~21 ores at level 1, ~103 at level 19.

Computed examples: coal → 12 % per ore; a 30-XP crop → 8 %; a fish → 70 % per catch if the JSON's 150 XP is used (with the 6.3.7 hard-coded 250 the roll exceeds 100 %, i.e. a guaranteed tiered fish).

**Stations and crafting**
- Station recipes (CTE2): Cooking Station = 8 planks around coal; Alchemy Station = 3 stone under 1 Spiritual Produce; Salvaging Station = 8 stone around a Common Stone; "Destroy Output" item = 8 Common Stones around a minecart — `DP/recipes/{cooking_station,alchemy_station,salvaging_station,destroy_output_exp}.json`. "Crafting Stations for each of the Professions are tied to the player that placed them" — `Q/professions.snbt`.
- Crafting is automatic: when inputs change the station crafts the selected or locked recipe repeatedly while materials last; pulling from adjacent chests exists but CTE2 disables it (`STATION_SUCK_NEARBY_CHESTS = false`) — `SRC/.../profession/ProfessionBlockEntity.java` (`tick`, `tryRecipe`), `CFG`.
- Gate: profession level ≥ the recipe tier's min level (1/20/40/60/80/100) — `ProfessionRecipe.getLevelRequirement`, lang `mmorpg.chat.prof_recipe_level_not_enough`.
- Recipe JSON (810 files): `{id, profession, tier 0–5, exp, mats:[{type:"ITEM", id, num}], result, result_num, set_tier_nbt}`; `set_tier_nbt` stamps the recipe tier on the output = its item level 1/20/40/60/80/100 — `DP/mmorpg_profession_recipe/`, `SRC/.../profession/LeveledItem.java`.
- Crafting XP = `clamp(skill, tierMin, tierMax) × difficulty × recipe.exp × profession-XP stat` (no ^1.1); difficulty by `skill − recipeLevel`: 0 → ×1.0; 1–10 "Hard" ×0.75 (5 % double output); 11–20 "Medium" ×0.5 (10 %); 21–30 "Easy" ×0.25 (25 %); >30 fell back to ×1.0 in 6.3.7 — `SRC/.../profession/ProfessionRecipe.java` (`getExpReward`, `RecipeDifficulty`); fixed in CTE2 2.1.0 ("crafting recipes more than 30 levels lower than you actually gave you more experience…") — `CL:443`.
- Computed pace (common tier-0 cooking/alchemy recipe, exp 100): 100 XP at skill 1 (≈4 crafts per level), 375 at 5, 750 at 10 (≈5 crafts/level), 950 at 19 (≈11 crafts/level); a tier-1 common at skill 20 gives 2,000 (≈6 crafts/level).
- "Destroy Output" (quest name "Disassembler's Learning Method"): voids the craft for more XP — ×2 in 6.3.7 code and the quest ("gain double Profession experience"), ×3 per the 6.3.14 lang ("give 3X the EXP") — `ProfessionBlockEntity.tryRecipe`, `Q/professions.snbt`, lang `mmorpg.chat.destroys_output` (conflict).
- Rarity is deterministic: the Stone you insert (`mmorpg:stone/0..5` = common…mythic) fixes output rarity; only random bonus = difficulty-based double output — `ProfessionRecipe.craft`.
- Cost/XP ladders common→mythic: Stone tier 0→5 and coins 1 copper / 5 copper / 1 iron / 5 iron / 1 gold / 1 emerald; XP 100/125/175/250/350/475 (cooking, alchemy), 100/150/250/400/600/850 (gear crafting, infusing), sharpening stones 90/120/150/200/300/425 — `DP/mmorpg_profession_recipe/` (e.g. `food_common_life0.json` … `food_mythic_life0.json`, `currency_sharpening_stone*.json`). "Profession Experience has been completely retuned - higher rarity crafts grant exponentially more experience" (2.1.0) — `CL:128`.
- Counts (all 810 parsed): alchemy 300 = 216 elixirs (6 stats × 6 rarities × 6 tiers) + 36 health + 36 mana potions + 5 minor + 5 major reset potions + 2 MCA gender potions; cooking 216 = 144 meals (4 × 6 × 6) + 72 seafood (2 × 6 × 6); gear crafting 150 = 144 gear souls (weapon/armor/jewelry/offhand × 6 × 6) + 6 sharpening stones; infusing 144 scrolls (4 × 6 × 6) — `DP/mmorpg_profession_recipe/`.
- Tier-0 common inputs: Life meal = 3 Spiritual Raw Meat + 2 cooked fish fillet + Common Stone + 1 copper coin; Mana meal = 3 Produce + 3 beetroot; Energy meal = 3 Produce + 3 carrot; Magic meal = 3 Meat + 4 cooked cod slice; Seafood EXP = 3 Raw Fish + 3 tropical fish; Seafood Loot = 3 Raw Fish + 3 pufferfish; elixirs Strength/Dexterity/Intelligence = 3 Produce + 3 potato/carrot/cabbage; Might/Arcane/Crit = 3 Raw Fish + 3 redstone / 5 lapis / 3 gold ingot; from tier 3 the flavour items become Blue Skies / modded items (scalefruit, cryo root, fiery beans, pyrope, moonstone, ventium ingot, corn, gysahl green, cooked venison, cooked crab, tuna, carp) — `DP/mmorpg_profession_recipe/{food,seafood,buff_potion}_common_*{0,3}.json`. Quest summary: cooking "Meat: Health/Magic Shield Regen | Produce: Mana/Energy Regen | Fish: EXP/Loot"; alchemy "Meat: Health/Resource Potion | Produce: Core Stat Potion | Fish: Damage Buff Potion" — `Q/professions.snbt`.
- Gear souls: e.g. common weapon soul tier 0 = 3 Spiritual Ore + 2 Common Stones + 2 iron ingots + stick + copper coin; Stone count rises with recipe tier (2/4/6/8/10/12); identifier item picks the slot (stick weapon, leather armor, string offhand, copper ingot jewelry); the metal changes per tier (iron → emerald → froststeel → regalium → horizonite → charoite) — `DP/mmorpg_profession_recipe/stat_soul_family_*`; 0.8.0 "the cost of Stone starts low and goes up as you increase in level instead of being based on rarity" — `ACL:2694`.
- Infusing: "When used on your Gear, you have a chance of applying a random stat … The Infusion has a chance of failure … Infusions must be applied in rarity order" — `Q/professions.snbt`. Sharpening stones "push quality above the normal cap of 20" — same file.
- Salvaging: each M&S gear piece → 1 Stone of its rarity; uniques → 2–9 random Stones; +1 % chance per salvaging level of a whole extra result; XP = `30 × rarity.item_tier_power × itemLevel` — `ProfessionBlockEntity.trySalvage`, `SRC/mine_and_slash/saveclasses/item_classes/GearItemData.java` (`getSalvageResult`), `SRC/mine_and_slash/uncommon/interfaces/data_items/ICommonDataItem.java`. Station salvaging gives 10× the XP of hub auto-salvage — `ACL:1465`; hopper-fed — `Q/professions.snbt`; 2.1.0 unique "sticker book" (salvaging a unique unlocks crafting it with account-bound Philosopher's Shards) — `CL:120`.

### Inferences
- Material flow: Mining → Ore → Gear Crafting + Infusing; Farming → Produce → mana/energy meals + Str/Dex/Int elixirs (+ the Alchemy Station recipe itself); Fishing → Raw Fish → seafood + Might/Arcane/Crit elixirs; Bounty Hunting → Raw Meat (shop) → life/magic meals + health/mana potions; Salvaging → Stones → every recipe; coins → every recipe. Salvaging is the hinge from combat loot to crafting.
- Tiers are really world-progress gates (ores/crops exist only in later dimensions), which maps directly onto Realmforge's realm chapters.
- Crafting XP grows linearly with skill while requirements grow ~^2.4, but the per-craft XP is large, so crafting stays at ≈4–12 crafts per level, while gathering needs dozens to 100+ actions per level late in a tier.

### Gaps
- M&S 6.3.14 code is not available locally: fishing XP source (150 vs 250), destroy-output ×2 vs ×3 and the >30-level fix are unverified in code.
- Mine and Slash Extras (Bounty Hunting XP per bounty, Profession Shop UI) is not in the local jars.

## 2. The Profession Shop (Bounty Slips) and rested profession XP

### Takeaway
Bounty Slips from Bountiful board tasks are a cross-profession currency: the shop sells 4–8 tier materials for 2/8/32 slips (tiers 0–2) and 1–16 Empowered Slips (tiers 3–5), plus rarity Stones, each gated by profession level. Rested XP cross-feeds combat → professions at 0.25 per XP point (and 0.05 back) and doubles gains until the pool is spent.

### Cited Findings
- "Added a new Profession Shop! You can access this with the menu wheel. Items in this shop will show up once you reach the correct profession level" (1.0.0) — `ACL:1455`; default key P — `Q/professions.snbt`.
- Shop table (cte2_instance/config/profession_shop.json): Raw Meat 8 per purchase for 2/8/32 Bounty Slips at bounty-hunting levels 1/20/40 and 1/8/16 Empowered Slips at 60/80/100; Ore, Produce, Raw Fish 4 per purchase for 2/8/32 slips (levels 1/20/40) and 1/4/16 Empowered Slips (60/80/100); salvaging: 21 Common Stones for 1 slip (lvl 1), 20 Uncommon for 8 (20), 9 Rare for 32 (40), 4 Epic for 1 Empowered (60), 1 Legendary for 2 Empowered (80), 1 Mythic for 16 Empowered (100); every entry has `requiredProfession` + `requiredLevel`.
- Slip sources: Bountiful reward entries give 1–32 Bounty Slips (reputation ≥1/15/30, `unitWorth` 125/100/75) and 1–16 Empowered Slips at reputation 45/60/75 (`unitWorth` 5000/4000/3000) — cte2_instance/config/openloader/data/cte_configuration/data/bountiful/bounty_pools/bountiful/_all_rews.json; "Empowered Bounty Slips will start appearing at 45 reputation!" — `Q/professions.snbt`; "Increased Bounty Slip rewards by double" (1.0.6) — `ACL:964`. Board objectives "range from Act I to Act V … decrees are more impactful and focused" — `ACL:1458`.
- Rested XP: each combat XP point adds 0.25 to a bonus-profession-XP pool; each profession XP point adds 0.05 to a bonus-combat-XP pool; when profession XP is gained, up to the same amount is drawn from the pool and added (gains doubled while it lasts); on death both pools ×0.75 — `SRC/mine_and_slash/capability/player/data/RestedExpData.java`, `PlayerProfessionsData.addExp`, `CFG` (`COMBAT_TO_PROFESSION_RESTED_XP_GENERATION = 0.25`, `PROFESSION_TO_COMBAT_RESTED_XP_GENERATION = 0.05`, `RESTED_XP_DEATH_PENALTY = 0.75`); death penalties only from `DEATH_PENALTY_START_LEVEL = 50` (`CFG`; M&S 5.5: "death penalty now only activates after level 25, for … rested xp loss" — `mns_src_1.20/changelogs/v.5.5.txt:52`).
- Player-facing text: "Mine and Slash Professions can sometimes be tedious to level... Did you know that gaining experience through combat gives you Rested Profession Experience? This experience pools and will be consumed when performing Profession activities…" — `Q/act_ii.snbt`; pool shown in the M&S Hub (lang `mmorpg.gui.rested_prof_exp`).
- Tuning history: 0.3.0 lowered combat→profession rested XP (`ACL:4636`); 0.5.1 death penalty 25 % instead of 50 % (`ACL:4074`); 0.6.0 lowered profession→combat rate (`ACL:3820`); 0.9.0 "Rested EXP death penalty is now 25% instead of 75%" (`ACL:2138`); 1.0.3 auto-salvaging no longer gives or uses rested XP (`ACL:1142`).

### Inferences
- Shop + rested XP are deliberate anti-grind valves: combat players buy other professions' inputs with bounty currency and get passive profession catch-up.

### Gaps
- Bounty Hunting XP per bounty and slips per bounty live in Mine and Slash Extras/Bountiful internals not available locally.

## 3. Food: which foods give stats, magnitudes and durations, stacking, Food Diversity

### Takeaway
CTE2 stacks three food layers: (1) M&S profession meals and seafood — 60-minute buffs, one Meal + one Seafood (+ one Elixir) at a time, strength set by rarity and recipe tier (tier-0 common ≈ +5.5 % max HP or +3.6 % XP); (2) Mine and Meals tags that make ~480 ordinary foods grant timed M&S stats in 14 flavour categories; (3) Food Diversity (Spice of Life: Onion) — permanent milestone bonuses for variety among the last 48 foods eaten.

### Cited Findings
**(1) Profession meals and seafood**
- Slots: `POTION` ("Elixir", 20×60×30 ticks = 30 min, Alchemy), `MEAL` (60 min, Cooking), `FISH` ("Seafood", 60 min, Cooking); the buff map is keyed by slot, so a second meal overwrites the first meal buff while the three slots coexist — `SRC/mine_and_slash/capability/player/data/PlayerBuffData.java`; lang "You can have One Consumable active per each type: Elixirs, Meals and Seafoods." (`mmorpg.item_tips.buff_consumable_info`), tooltip "Duration: %1$s minutes".
- Use fails ("[Food Eat Fail]") if the item level (1/20/40/60/80/100 from recipe tier) exceeds the player's level — `PlayerBuffData.tryAdd`, lang `mmorpg.chat.eat_food_error`.
- Meals/seafood are food: nutrition 6, saturation modifier 5, 32-tick use time (elixirs drink in 32 ticks) — `SRC/.../profession/items/CraftedBuffFoodItem.java`.
- Value = `min + (max − min) × (rarityMid + itemLevel)/100`; rarityMid = middle of the rarity's `stat_percents` (common 8, uncommon 26, rare 43, epic 60, legendary 77, mythic 93); no clamp, so high tiers exceed `max` — `SRC/.../profession/buffs/StatBuff.java` (`getStats` passes `perc + lvl`), `SRC/mine_and_slash/saveclasses/ExactStatData.java`, `SRC/mine_and_slash/mmorpg/registers/common/items/CraftedRarity.java`, `DP/mmorpg_gear_rarity/*.json`.
- CTE2 ranges (`DP/mmorpg_stat_buff/*.json`): Life meal +5–10 % max health, +5–25 % health regen; Mana meal +5–10 % mana, +5–25 % mana regen; Energy meal +5–10 % energy, +5–25 % energy regen; Magic meal +7.5–15 % magic shield, +2–10 % MS regen; Seafood EXP +3–10 % bonus XP and +2–10 % total damage; Seafood Loot +2–10 % item quantity and +2–10 % total damage. M&S defaults were stronger: meals also had +20–50 out-of-combat regen and +1–3 flat regen; seafood +3–15 % XP/quantity and +5–25 % damage — `JAR/data/mmorpg/mmorpg_stat_buff/*.json`, `SRC/.../profession/buffs/StatBuffs.java`.
- Computed (common / rare / mythic): Life meal tier 0 (level 1) +5.5 / 7.2 / 9.7 % HP and +6.8 / 13.8 / 23.8 % HP regen; tier 1 (level 20) +6.4 / 8.2 / 10.7 % HP; tier 5 (level 100) +10.4 / 12.2 / 14.7 % HP; Seafood EXP tier 0 +3.6 / 6.1 / 9.6 % XP (+2.7 / 5.5 / 9.5 % damage), tier 5 +10.6 / 13.0 / 16.5 % XP.
- Quests: "You can cook foods which will provide hunger and saturation, but also provide stat boosts" — `Q/professions.snbt`; "Need a bit of an EXP boost? Consider eating Experience Gain Seafood crafted through Professions!" — `Q/act_iv.snbt`.

**(2) Mine and Meals — "all foods give stats"**
- 1.0.0: "All foods in CTE2 now give some form of Mine and Slash stat when eaten for an extended period of time!" — `ACL:1215`; 1.0.7 compat for "a wide variety of foods (including Cuisine Delight)" — `ACL:919`; 1.1.0 beverages from Brewin' And Chewin' and Farmer's Respite — `ACL:693`; "Added descriptions to all the new compat food buffs" — `ACL:1111`.
- Mine and Meals (mod id `mine_and_meals`, author Baketsu, jar 0.60): 28 effects = 14 categories × Ⅰ/Ⅱ (dairy, tea, sugar, strange, ice, alcohol, fruit, meat, grain, fish, vegetable, spice, sparkling, fried) and 70 item tags `<category><1|2>_<sub-level>` — cte2_jars/mine_and_meals-0.60/assets/mine_and_meals/lang/en_us.json, .../data/mine_and_meals/tags/items/. ## 4. Potions: which exist, effects, cooldowns, level ranges, quick-consume; vanilla brewing

### Takeaway

## Relevance to Realmforge

### Adopt
- **Three consumable slots, one active each, timer in real time:** Elixir (Alchemy, 30 min), Meal (Cooking, 60 min), plus one more food slot (CTE2: Seafood, 60 min); same-slot replaces, different slots stack (`PlayerBuffData`). This directly delivers "weak but long-lasting" buffs that never stack into mandatory DPS. Keep the MobEffect-style icon with remaining time.
- **CTE2 tier-0 common magnitudes as the M1 baseline:** roughly +5–6 % of one stat (STR/DEX/INT, physical or spell damage, max HP) or +3–4 % XP for 30–60 min, from 3 gathered materials + 3 common crops + 1 cheap catalyst. That is exactly the owner's "+5 % damage for an hour"; scale about +1–2 percentage points per chapter instead of CTE2's 6 rarities.
- **Instant %-of-max health/mana potions with a shared per-type cooldown (10 s), 4 per craft, plus a quick-consume key that drinks the best potion the player may use.** Note that CTE2's default key Y collides with Realmforge's spell-bar key Y (R T F G C V X Y), so pick another default (e.g. H or a mouse button).
- **Profession level ≤ character level** and **one material tier per chapter** (CTE2: tiers every 20 levels, tied to dimensions). This fits 10-level chapters and realm unlocks: chapter-1 tier = Overworld + stone realm ores, chapter 2 = desert/tundra materials, and so on.
- **Deterministic, data-driven recipes** (`id, profession, tier, exp, inputs, result, count, set item level`) crafted in a station with recipe lock and bulk crafting. No RNG rarity rolls fits the owner's dislike of RNG grind. Write our own format and names.
- **Anti-grind valves:** rested profession XP fed by combat (CTE2: 0.25 per combat XP, doubles gains until spent) and a contract-currency shop that sells other professions' inputs at profession-level gates. These map onto the roadmap's job board and profession orders and let one player run several professions without grinding each.
- **Separate time scales and data types** for consumable buffs (minutes to hours) and combat effects (seconds; CTE2 3–30 s), so consumables never interact with proc stacking. The planned "tenacity" stat (shorter negative effects) belongs only to the combat-effect layer.

### Adapt
- **Recipe volume:** CTE2's 810 hand-copied JSONs (6 tiers × 6 rarities × families) produced recurring recipe bugs. Realmforge should generate tiered variants with datagen or a single recipe with a tier parameter, use about 3 quality steps instead of 6 rarities, and unit-test the formulas.
- **Rarity input:** CTE2 sets rarity with salvaged Stones, which ties consumables to the gear-loot loop. For early-game food and potions, let quality come from realm materials (e.g. a stone-realm gem dust as the catalyst) so that a level-1 player can craft from Overworld and stone-realm drops alone.
- **Mining as the M1 profession:** copy the structure, not the numbers. Each new stone-realm ore gets its own XP value (CTE2: 10–80 per ore by tier), there is a 10–20 % base chance of a realm crafting material scaled by XP, and pickaxe levelling grants an affix. CTE2 grants an affix every 20 levels; with 10-level chapters, every 5 levels fits better. A daily "boosted materials" rotation (M&S has the message but it is disabled in code) is a cheap way to vary gathering.
- **Rework of vanilla brewing:** CTE2 left vanilla brewing alone and put all RPG potions in a separate instant station, and it removed Nether Wart from healing potions early. Because Realmforge removes the Nether and End, define potion bases and fire reagents from realm-1 and Overworld materials (stand-ins for nether wart and blaze powder) and decide whether brewing keeps a timed brew process or uses instant station crafting.
- **Farmer's Delight rebalancing via categories:** follow the Mine and Meals pattern of tagging each food with 1–2 flavour categories that map to one stat each, with a strength grade by dish complexity (raw → cooked → composed → feast). CTE2 tagged 479 foods with 823 entries. Realmforge should publish exact values and durations in tooltips, which Mine and Meals hides.
- **Food diversity:** reward variety over a rolling window (CTE2: last 48 foods with recency decay; diversity ≈ nutrition/5 × saturation/0.6). Define stacking explicitly as additive per milestone and show it in a food-book UI. Weight crafted buff meals separately so that they don't dominate the score (each CTE2 crafted meal is worth 10 diversity).
- **XP curve:** CTE2 reuses the character curve (389 XP for level 2, ~11k by level 20) with ^1.1 action scaling. Realmforge should tune its own curve so that one chapter's profession levels take about as long as that chapter's content, with crafting at ≈5–10 crafts per level and gathering at a few dozen actions per level.

### Avoid
- Long gathering grinds late in a tier (CTE2: 100+ ores per level near the end of a tier). The pack itself calls professions "tedious to level", and the owner quit CTE2 over grind.
- Too many inputs per craft: CTE2 uses a tier material, a rarity Stone, a flavour item and a coin for every recipe, across coins in 4 metals. Early-game consumables should need 2–3 inputs.
- Silent formula quirks: unclamped "rarity% + item level" rolls above the max, the >30-level XP fallback bug, the ×2 vs ×3 destroy-output mismatch, and SoL max-merging that made CTE2's repeated +1 milestones non-cumulative. Specify stacking and caps in data and test them.
- XP-sink mechanics that encourage mindless crafting, such as CTE2's Destroy Output item (void the item for double XP).
- Making consumables effectively mandatory or tied to endgame loot. CTE2 cut its consumable values in 2.0.0 and still had to lower potion cooldowns to get players using them. Keep them cheap, optional and long-lasting, as the owner wants.
