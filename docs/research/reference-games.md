# Borrow the loops, fill the worlds

> Reference-game research of 2026-10-12 (RLM-94): Craft to Exile 2 on Mine and Slash, Advent of Ascension and Hytale with its dungeon mods, read against Matěj's realm, dungeon, spell and consumable decisions of 11–12 October 2026. Index and caveats: [README](README.md). Mechanics and numbers only; never reuse names, assets or code of the reference games.

Matěj's decisions of 11–12 October 2026 hold up against all three reference games; what the games add is tested loops to copy and failure modes to design against. **Advent of Ascension (AoA) 3.x** already shipped the realm skeleton as decided: one portal frame, a key per realm that is never consumed, a colour per realm, unlocks chained from the previous realm. It is also the clearest evidence of what makes a realm feel dead: every 1.12.2 dimension was **a single biome**, silent and mostly hostile, with **one point of interest per ~26–212 chunks**, and **15 of 22 dimensions had no passive animal**. AoA's own 1.21 rebuild of Precasia (13 biomes, ~30 placed features each, herds) is the fix. **Craft to Exile 2 (CTE2) on Mine and Slash (MnS)** provides the data shapes and calibration numbers for level bands, buff slots, room-kit instances and wave encounters. Its 2.4-power XP curve, **170 class spells**, **743 named stats**, **810 hand-written recipes** and stacked map currencies produced the grind Matěj quit over, and the "drop off in engagement at 55" its author lists first among his problems. **Hytale** shows how one theme stays varied (a zone splits into 3 regions of 3–7 biomes, with a friendly and a hostile faction, so far in place in the first two zones, and tiered settlement families), and its two leading dungeon mods show the instance flow players reward: crafted key → temporary portal → miniboss keys → boss → treasure room, with the next key guaranteed and a vendor as fallback. Three additions to the plan matter most: **a per-realm budget for life and structures with a measured density target**, **one data schema for all encounters** (boss chains and wave arenas), and **a deterministic source for every progression item**. Recommendations are numbered per area (RE-9 is item 9 under Realms) so Jira tickets can quote them.

## 1. Realms (RE): one frame and many keys work, single-biome worlds do not

AoA already shipped Matěj's portal design. The frame takes 13 Ancient Rock, four carved rune blocks and a Rune of Power, with a 3×4 interior. Right-clicking it with a dimension's realmstone lights it. The realmstone is **never consumed** and stacks to 1. Each dimension has its own portal colour and a one-line activation message, and each player remembers where they came from (AoA 1.12.2 source: CarvedRunicPortalBlock, PortalBlock, PlayerDataManager). Players hit three snags: building the first frame (an NPC ended up selling the runes for 20 copper coins each), clearing a frame with a blank stone before re-keying it, and obscure unlock tasks. What sank AoA's realms was their content, not the portal; Hytale's zones show the opposite model, and CTE2 shows how to turn a world into a level band.

### Adopt

1. **One portal structure, realm items that are never consumed, a colour per realm.** Copy AoA's rules: the item is not consumed, stacks to 1, and lighting an already lit portal needs no key. Each realm gets one colour and particle set and one flavour line (AoA 1.12.2 source, CarvedRunicPortalBlock). Improve on AoA: the selector block switches destination directly, with no clearing step.
2. **Per-player return memory.** On each crossing, store the origin realm and position. The portal inside realm X leads back to where the player came from, finds or builds the matching portal within a search radius, and keeps coordinates 1:1 (AoA 1.12.2 source, AoATeleporter). Plan tests for the cases AoA kept fixing: void worlds, ceilings and portal loops ("Re-wrote the portal search & generation algorithms", AoA 3.2.5 changelog).
3. **A realm works like a Hytale zone: three sub-regions of rising tier.** Hytale splits each zone into 3 regions of 3–7 biomes, plus lake, valley, canyon and village variants. Its desert alone covers savanna, oasis badlands, tar pits and a mushroom desert ([Zone](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Zone), [Howling Sands](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Howling_Sands)). Map the sub-regions to the chapter's sub-bands (e.g. 10–13 / 13–17 / 17–20).
4. **The realm's level band as data, plus an "enraged" band for revisits.** CTE2 stores, per dimension, a min and max level, a strength multiplier and a second range that applies once the player has outgrown the first: Overworld 1–15 → 50–70, Nether 10–30 → 55–75 (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_dimension). It was added as "Something players have been asking for" (CTE2 changelog 0.9.1), and it keeps finished persistent realms worth a visit.
5. **One identity element per realm, visible on its mobs.** CTE2 gives each dimension an elemental affinity: Nether fire, Undergarden chaos, Everbright cold and lightning (CTE2 quests, "Mob Affinities"). Hytale builds each zone around one element: earth, wind, water, fire ([Zone](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Zone)). Show the element in mob design and drops, not only as a stat conversion.
6. **One signature environmental rule per realm, with a counter that progression unlocks.** AoA's Lelyetia pushes players back up from below (an artifact cancels it), Shyrelands has wind gusts on high ground, Vox Ponds drains health until a Hazmat suit is worn, and Lunalus later gained low gravity and an oxygen need (AoA 1.12.2 and 1.21 source, dimension event handlers). Every realm AoA rebuilt kept or gained such a rule. Pair it with a safe arrival pocket: AoA 1.21 builds a radius-3 air bubble around the underwater realm's portal (AoA 1.21 source, LboreanPortalBlock).
7. **The realm map as data.** AoA 1.21 stores its unlock screen as one JSON file per realm, with `gui_x`, `gui_y`, `parents` and hover text for the unlock condition (AoA 1.21 assets, realmstonegui). Use the same shape for Realmforge's realm map, and mark the optional side realms.
8. **Music and ambience per realm from the first release.** All of AoA's 1.12.2 dimensions shipped silent (`NULL_MUSIC`); 1.21 adds music per biome (AoA 1.12.2 source, Enums; AoA 1.21 biome data). Hytale ships zone soundtracks, including 24 new Zone 4 tracks, and cave music that crossfades between layers ([Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)).

### Adapt

9. **Craft the next realm's item from the realm the player is leaving.** AoA's best unlock tasks use the parent realm's content: a Deep Crystal used on a Deeplands Case Construct unlocks Crystevia. Its worst are hidden puzzles, such as "throw a Blank Realmstone into a carrot crop in Precasia, then defeat your creation", which needed a wiki and many bug fixes (AoA 1.12.2 lang, realmstone menu; AoA 3.2.2 changelog). Craft the next realm item from the current realm's ore or gem plus its boss's guaranteed drop, and show the recipe in the quest log.
10. **M1 stone realm: the Deeplands' scale without its sameness.** AoA's Deeplands is the closest precedent: a flat floor at y 20 under a rock underside at about y 61–65, between unbreakable layers, so space is not the problem (AoA 1.12.2 source, Deeplands chunk generator). It feels barren because it has one stone block ("Deep Rock") and one walking layer, one level-12 light per chunk, no music, a timid 5-HP crab as the only non-hostile creature, about 24 structures per km² with no loot chest, and its ores at y 70–114, above the floor, mixed with 25 trap veins of 20 blocks per chunk. For Realmforge: several floor heights, ledges, chasms and multi-level halls; ore bands in the walls and pillars of walkable layers; lit landmarks; oases and water.
11. **Depth as sub-bands.** Hytale's underground jungle stacks four levels: a fiery transition, a sparse level with outposts, a lush level with villages, and a deep, bioluminescent level with hostile dinosaurs ([Tropical Jungle](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Tropical_Jungle)). In the stone realm, map depth layers to level sub-bands. Crystevia-style shelves and ceiling waterfalls (1 chunk in 10) can make one crystal-grotto sub-zone. Note that AoA removed glowing crystal blocks for performance (AoA 3.1.2 changelog).
12. **Desert (M4): AoA's 1.21 Barathos plus Hytale's oasis.** Barathos in 1.12 had no vegetation at all, and players said it "feels like more of a template than it's own dimension" ([GitHub #1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)). The 1.21 rework has 4 biomes (flats, barren oasis, burning pits, stone spears), fossil sites, resource pits at spacing 28 and a sandstorm world event (AoA 1.21 worldgen data). Hytale's oasis is a sunken basin with water, rare flowers and fauna found nowhere else, such as flamingos ([Oasis](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Oasis)).
13. **Tundra (M4): Hytale's frozen zone.** Lakes with islands, glacier lakes, hot-spring plains, stilt villages with boats, coastal ice camps, multi-level mines, and yeti caves marked by ice spikes and a campfire ([Whisperfrost Frontiers](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Whisperfrost_Frontiers), [Outlander Village](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Outlander_Village), [Yeti](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Yeti)). Hytale has nothing under its ice and keeps shipwrecks in the deep ocean, so under-ice content and frozen shipwrecks would be Realmforge's own ideas.
14. **Fire realm (late): a volcano you climb down into.** Hytale's volcano is a hollow mountain with lava on its sides and a shaft "almost all the way to bedrock" into a hidden jungle. A treasure pile (the "Fire Dragon's Lair") at the bottom shows as a yellow spot on the map, but the dragon never spawns ([Volcano](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Volcano)). Put a grounded dragon arena there, and fill the surface with burnt or undead variants of the realm's animals ([Devastated Lands](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Devastated_Lands)).
15. **Sky islands: big and close together.** AoA's 1.12 Haven had End-style islands; 3.5 switched to "massive", mountainous islands that "generate fairly close together" ([The Haven](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/The_Haven)). Add a void rescue, like Endgame & QoL's "The Void pulls you back" with no penalty ([Void Realm](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/void-realm)).
16. **Decide respawn per realm.** Every AoA 1.12.2 dimension forbids respawning there (`canRespawnHere = false`; AoA 1.12.2 source, WorldProvider classes). Persistent realms need an explicit rule, for example respawn at the realm's hub camp (see open question 1).

### Avoid

17. **Single-biome, silent realms with a frozen sky.** AoA 1.12.2 had one biome per dimension, no music, and permanent noon in most realms (AoA 1.12.2 source). The community verdict: "every dimension is super simple, boring, and empty" ([#1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)).
18. **Darkness and hidden information as a theme.** 6 of AoA's 11 A–G dimensions have no skylight. Greckon's mobs use screen overlays, invisibility and muted sound (AoA 1.12.2 source). Players lumped three dark realms together as an "evil dark place with scary mobs you have a hard time seeing" ([#1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)). This conflicts with Matěj's no-horror rule.
19. **Ambient damage with no early counter.** Vox Ponds deals 1 point of damage that ignores armour every tick until the player wears a full Hazmat set (AoA 1.12.2 source, VoxPondsEvents). A realm's counter-item must be cheap and arrive with the realm.
20. **Duplicate themes and single loud palettes.** "candyland is an assault on the eyes and Celeve is a monstrosity"; players proposed merging the three dark realms ([#1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)).
21. **Realms that are only level gates.** A CTE2 player wrote: "after a while you just power straight through all the different dimensions" ([review 4142](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/4142)).
22. **Terrain tied to the chunk grid or to generation order.** Shyrelands is one 16×16 walled courtyard per chunk. Runandor's plateau height comes from a counter that grows with each generated chunk. Mysterium is a chessboard of mounds (AoA 1.12.2 source). AoA replaced these layouts in 3.5.

## 2. Dungeons and instances (DU): finish at the boss, guarantee the keys, run every fight on one engine

CTE2's Dungeon Realm shows how the plumbing works. A consumable map item, started on a crafted device, sends the party to a grid slot in one bedrock-filled dimension. There the game assembles 18–24 rooms from one of about 21 themed room kits. Command blocks in the rooms spawn mobs and chests when a player comes within 2–3 chunks ([Dungeon Realm datapacks](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/dungeon-realm/latest/docs/datapacks.md)). Two parts hurt. A map completes by kill percentage, which raises its reward grade; this broke over and over when mobs despawned. And the author named the boss teleporter (usable from 30% kills) a "major pain point" (CTE2 changelog 2.0.0). Hytale and its dungeon mods use a cleaner key loop. Wave encounters appear in all four sources in almost the same shape.

### Adopt

1. **One instance dimension, with plots handed out by a counter.** Dungeon Realm gives each new map the next grid slot in one dimension that is "not meant to be built in" and can be wiped (Dungeon Realm source, MapDeviceBlock; CTE2 config). AoA's Nowhere places its arenas at fixed coordinates in one non-natural dimension ([Nowhere](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Nowhere)). This is the design of [ADR 0019](../decisions/0019-dungeon-instances-in-plots.md) and spike 3, and both games show it scales.
2. **Hytale's portal robustness rules.** The key is consumed when the portal opens. The portal vanishes if nobody enters within 90 s. The key is refunded if the instance fails to generate or has no valid spawn. The portal cannot be closed while someone is inside. The instance is deleted from disk when it ends. Everyone is ejected if all players die. Sources: [Ancient Gateway](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Ancient_Gateway), [Update 4](https://hytale.com/news/2026/3/hytale-patch-notes-update-4), [Update 6](https://hytale.com/news/2026/8/update-6-patch-notes), [Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7). These belong in W4's "done when".
3. **Room kits with six slot types and text markers.** A Dungeon Realm kit lists rooms for six slots: entrance, straight hallway, curved hallway, T-junction, crossing and dead end, each at most 16×16. Text markers in the rooms create the content:
   - `mob` spawns 2–3 mobs and `pack` 4–6;
   - `elite`, `boss` and `exit` place what their names say;
   - `chest` always places a chest, and `chance_chest` places one at 25%, at most once per room.

   The docs put it simply: "you pretty much only need to know about: mob, mob_horde, chest and chance_chest" ([datapacks](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/dungeon-realm/latest/docs/datapacks.md)). This matches a `content/rooms/` format and Matěj building key rooms by hand.
4. **Instance rules.** From Dungeon Realm: no breaking or placing blocks; mobs spawn once, never respawn, cannot grief and take no environmental damage; teleport items are banned; an exit block sits at the entrance ([Dungeon Realm intro](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/dungeon-realm/latest/docs/intro.md)). From the Hytale mods: players who fall off are rescued with no penalty, NPCs cannot hurt each other, and the inventory is always kept on a voluntary exit ([Void Realm](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/void-realm), [Instance Config](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/instance-config)).
5. **The core mod owns return points.** CTE2 needed KubeJS scripts to remember where a player entered and to find the boss room (CTE2 1.1.3 KubeJS: return_script, boss_teleport_locator). Build the entry position, the return on exit or death and a home fallback into `InstanceManager`.
6. **The boss drops the key to the treasure room.** Major Dungeons' final boss drops the key to a treasure room that holds the exit and a vendor ([Dungeon of Fear I](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/dungeon-of-fear-i)). That gives "the instance is complete when its final boss dies" a clear moment. The dungeon type without a boss ends in its treasury.
7. **Miniboss keys open the boss door.** In Dungeon of Fear III, two minibosses drop the red keys that open the way on. Locked-door blocks consume the key and, on a failed use, name the key that is missing ([DoF III](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/dungeon-of-fear-i-i-i), [Locked Doors](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/locked-doors-and-keys)). This is Matěj's miniboss → miniboss → boss shape, with no puzzle.
8. **One wave engine with an explicit state machine.** Endgame & QoL runs every wave fight through COUNTDOWN (3 s) → SPAWNING → ACTIVE → WAVE_CLEAR → INTERVAL (8 s) → COMPLETED or FAILED. Its trials have 5 waves within 4:30–9:00, a boss in the final wave, and a 50-block leash that warns instead of failing ([Warden Trials](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/warden-trials)). Take the wave data from CTE2: a mob list per wave, +5% mob health and damage per wave (capped at +20%), a longer setup before the boss wave, a failure penalty, mobs leashed to 16 blocks (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, gateways), and one new announced modifier per wave over 3–5 waves of 25 mobs ([Obelisk docs](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/ancient-obelisks/latest/docs/intro.md)).
9. **Pay by the last wave reached.** Hytale's Coffer Event keeps chests locked until the end and pays by the wave reached: wave 1 gives 20 scraps and 1 crystal shard; wave 5 gives 120 scraps, 8 shards, 3 Voidhearts and a new key ([The Guttering Hollows](https://hytalewiki.org/w/The_Guttering_Hollows)). Obelisks add a 1% chest chance per kill, times modifiers, capped at 20 chests (Ancient Obelisks source). The instance completes when its last wave is cleared, but a failed run still pays for the waves it cleared.
10. **Place loot by risk and secrecy.** Endgame & QoL's Frozen Dungeon grades its chests by where they stand: Common near the entrance, Rare in the tower, Epic in hidden and underwater side areas, Legendary in the boss zone ([Frozen Dungeon](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/frozen-dungeon)). Exploring pays without puzzles.
11. **One data file per dungeon.** Major Dungeons defines the key, portal type, time limit, modes, drop lists, locked doors and mimic chests in JSON ([Modder Tutorials](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/modder-tutorials)). A Realmforge dungeon YAML should declare the key item, the level sub-range, the boss list (0–3), chest tiers, modes and splash text. The portal screen shows the name, the recommended sub-range and the boss count, and can float a preview of the destination, as Major Dungeons does ([Instance Config](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/instance-config)).

### Adapt

12. **Complete at the boss, with at most a light clear bonus.** CTE2's kill-percentage ladder (Common at 0% up to Mythic at 75%, 2–4 reward chests) forced full clears. It broke whenever mobs despawned or unloaded, with fixes from 0.9.3b to 2.1.0 (CTE2 changelog; Dungeon Realm source, DungeonMapData). Keep Matěj's rule. At most, add "cleared 80% or more → +1 chest".
13. **Key modifiers instead of a pile of affixes.** CTE2 maps roll from 67 affixes and 26 opt-in curses, on top of tier and rarity (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_map_affix). Give a key 0–2 readable modifiers, for example "Stone Skin: mobs +20% armour, +1 chest", rolled or chosen at the NPC.
14. **Pick the difficulty at the portal.** Major Dungeons picks a mode before entry. Its Hardcore mode sets mob health ×3, damage ×2 and boss health ×4, and swaps in better drop lists and chest spawners ([Instance Config](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/instance-config)). Keep the picker and the better drop lists; drop the item loss.
15. **No hard timer on boss dungeons.** Hytale's portal worlds last 5–12 minutes ([Fragment](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Fragment)). Major Dungeons gives 30–40 minutes, and staying past the limit kills the player ([DoF I](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/dungeon-of-fear-i)). Keep rising pressure only for wave instances, in soft stages.
16. **Handcrafted for story, procedural for repeats, several kits per realm.** Major Dungeons and Endgame & QoL ship fixed handcrafted instances that players praise for their bosses. YUNG's mod, with a single procedural layout, drew "Right now there's one dungeon type" ([HostHavoc](https://hosthavoc.com/blog/hytales-most-popular-mods)). CTE2 runs about 21 room kits and still added a filter to skip disliked layouts (CTE2 changelog 2.1.0). Chest spawners that roll a chest or a mimic when the room generates (75/25) add cheap variety ([Mimic Blocks](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/mimic-blocks)).
17. **Open-world dungeons: chained prefabs and a respawn rule.** Hytale chains prefab pieces; each ends in a staircase or ladder that the next piece continues ([visual tour of zone 3](https://hytale.com/news/2019/4/a-visual-tour-of-zone-3)). Some copies get optional rooms that others lack ([Shale Dungeon](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Shale_Dungeon)). For respawning, either CTE2's rule (the structure regenerates 96 h after the last activity if fewer than ~200 blocks changed and it lies over 200 blocks from a respawn point; CTE2 config) or Hytale's (mobs return once the player leaves far enough and comes back).
18. **Timed defence events in the open world.** In AoA, a Rune Templar loses its 400 HP over 80 s while adds spawn, at most 4 at a time, and pays loot when it reaches 0 (AoA 1.12.2 source, EntityRuneTemplar). That is a puzzle-free encounter for open-world sites. Place side encounters inside dungeons rarely, as CTE2 places its timed arena blocks inside maps.
19. **Gauntlets without fetch rooms.** AoA's Immortallis alternated rooms like "collect gold, deposit it" and kill-25 quotas with four boss duels ([Immortallis](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Immortallis)). Its Primordial Five chain five bosses with 6,550 HP in total (AoA 1.12.2 source). Keep the chain shape, with combat rooms and breathers in between.

### Avoid

20. **Dungeons only in the endgame.** CTE2 maps drop from level 55 (50 in 2.x). The author lists "the drop off in engagement at 55 (maps)" first among his pain points (CTE2 changelog 2.0.0).
21. **Dungeon worlds with one shared copy.** AoA built Ancient Cavern and Immortallis once at chunk (0,0); its own log line asks "Is this about to cause some 'cascading worldgen' warnings in the console? Yes." (AoA 1.12.2 source). Both were removed in 3.5 ([3.5 Beta 2](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/blob/1.16.5/changelogs/3.5-Beta-2.md)).
22. **Item loss, and death when time runs out.** Major Dungeons' Hardcore wipes the inventory ("You've Lost Everything..."), and staying past the time limit "results in death" ([Instance Config](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/instance-config), [DoF I](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/dungeon-of-fear-i)).
23. **Required parkour and fetch objectives.** Dungeon of Fear III's green key comes from "a Parkour challenge" ([DoF III](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/dungeon-of-fear-i-i-i)).
24. **Encounters that spawn from kills.** CTE2's act portals opened when mobs died, and a reviewer complained of "clutter mob / item management" ([review 566](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/566)).
25. **Two-step entry items.** CTE2 removed the in-between map item for its side arenas because players found it clunky (CTE2 changelog 1.1.0). One key, one portal.

## 3. Bosses and mobs (BM): grounded arenas, readable phases, short formulas

AoA's 35 bosses are summoned on demand at altars inside landmark structures. For example, Kror (2,200 HP, 60 melee damage) needs a Charging Table placed on the Kror Altar inside the Kror Pillars, then a Boulder Dash. Boss health runs from 500 to 4,000 (AoA 1.12.2 source). The rewrite moved every fight into instanced arenas. The developer's reasons: Minecraft "makes it incredibly easy to defeat a boss in 'cheap' ways", other mobs interfere, bosses fall into holes, and drops get destroyed ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)). For mobs, CTE2's rarity tiers are cheap variety, while its stacked scaling factors are a warning, and Hytale shows that small behaviours make creatures feel alive.

### Adopt

1. **Bosses fight in protected arenas.** AoA's current loop gives a 5 s warning, teleports the player to a free arena, gives 5 s to prepare, queues the fight when all arenas are busy, and returns bosses that fall into the void. Loot goes straight to the inventory, and boss health rises **+25% per extra player** ([AoA wiki: Bosses](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses)). Each arena is a data record: structure id, arena position, player spawns, boss spawns (AoA 1.21 source, NowhereBossArena).
2. **Conventions for multi-phase bosses.** From Endgame & QoL ([Bosses & Elites](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/bosses-elites)):
   - phases change at health thresholds, with 3 s of invulnerability at each transition;
   - heavy area attacks are telegraphed and stagger the player for 1 s;
   - the boss picks a new target every 8 s, weighted;
   - a one-time chat hint appears when the player uses a damage type the boss is immune to;
   - a boss bar shows within 50 blocks, hides beyond 55, and only one shows at a time.

   These serve W5's phase machine.
3. **Mob rarity tiers with geometric weights and modest multipliers.** CTE2 weights mob rarities 1024/256/64/16/4/1 from common to mythic. Health is multiplied ×1–3.5, damage ×1–1.75 and XP and loot ×1–8.75; epic mobs appear from level 10, legendary from 25, mythic from 50. Rare mobs carry affixes from a list of 25, such as Massive (+25% more health, 50% knockback resistance), Swift (+50% speed) and Vampire (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_mob_rarity, mmorpg_mob_affix). An elite should be a fight, not a wall.
4. **Per-mob overrides as data.** CTE2 keeps 167 records per mob, 11 per mod and 2 per mob type: health, damage, XP and loot multipliers, a forced rarity, a minimum level, an element conversion. Animals are fixed at level 1 and give no XP (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_entity).
5. **Stat bands per chapter.** AoA's median mob health climbs from ~30 in the Overworld to 55–77 at tier 1, 70–95 at tier 2, 115–127 at tier 3 and ~165 at tier 4, and damage from 4 to ~7, ~9–12, ~13–15 and 16. The player has 20 HP, plus at most 20 from a skill (AoA 1.12.2 source; medians computed). Take the shape of the ladder for chapter bands; Realmforge's own player health scale sets the absolute numbers.
6. **Small, readable behaviour rules for every faction and animal.** Hytale has plenty:
   - its tree-folk villagers grow wary when the player carries an axe near the village;
   - its orc-like sentries throw spears at sparrows and spar with each other, and the loser naps;
   - its swamp stalkers can be baited with raw fish, fear torches at night and copy frogs;
   - wolves flank as a pack and retreat when hurt, and predators hunt prey.

   Sources: [Kweebec](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Kweebec), [Trork](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Trork), [Fen Stalker](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Fen_Stalker), [Update 4](https://hytale.com/news/2026/3/hytale-patch-notes-update-4). Each one is a Minecraft AI goal.
7. **Status icons that can be read at a glance.** AoA shows icons over mobs for each immunity (melee, ranged, magic, fire, explosions, potions) and a mark for special mechanics (AoA 1.12.2 guides, creature icons). CTE2 replaces the level with a skull when a mob is 10 or more levels above the player (CTE2 config).
8. **Guardians that respawn and drop a guaranteed gem.** Hytale's temple golems stand still until a player comes within 10 blocks, return to their post when the player retreats 10–15 blocks, and respawn after one in-game day. Each drops the gem that unlocks the next tier of the alchemy bench ([Golem](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Golem), [Alchemist's Workbench](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Alchemist%27s_Workbench)). This fits realm landmarks and station tiers.
9. **Elite versions of cleared bosses.** AoA lets the player summon an "Elite" version of a beaten boss with a gold trophy; the summoning item is not consumed ([AoA wiki: Bosses](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses)). That is replay content with no new assets.

### Adapt

10. **Open-world bosses stay in their place.** AoA's altar inside a landmark, together with a summon item crafted from local materials, keeps each boss in its world (AoA 1.12.2 source, KrorAltar, PowerStation). CTE2 lets players re-summon bosses at their home structures with rings of blocks (CTE2 epilogue quests). Use a ritual at the boss's home, never a portable summon.
11. **Animated statues from AoA's construct family.** Crystevia's seven constructs each have one role: strength (15 damage), ranged, flying terror, speed, armour (15), flight and mind. But all of them regenerate 0.1 HP per tick (AoA 1.12.2 source). Keep one clear role per variant and drop the constant regeneration. Rock mobs that climb, and a timid critter that flees into the rock, fit the stone realm.
12. **Alternating vulnerability without flight.** Endgame & QoL's frost dragon swaps melee and projectile immunity between phases ([Bosses & Elites](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/bosses-elites)). AoA's Rockrider switches every 300 ticks between a form that ignores guns and one that ignores melee (AoA 1.12.2 source). For grounded bosses: a telegraphed shield that only one spell school can break.
13. **Choose one party scaling rule.** AoA adds +25% boss health per extra player; Endgame & QoL adds +50% (`1 + (n−1) × 0.5`) ([AoA wiki: Bosses](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses), [Bosses & Elites](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/bosses-elites)). Pick one for the command-based party system, and do not scale bosses to the player's level.
14. **Mob levels fixed by area, with a level-gap penalty.** CTE2 sets a mob's level from the nearest player (the lowest in a party) ±1, clamped to the dimension's band (CTE2 config; MnS source, LevelUtils). Realmforge fixes the band per sub-region and dungeon instead. Keep CTE2's penalty: no change within 3 levels, then −33% loot per extra level down to a floor of 25%, and mobs above the player hit harder (CTE2 config).
15. **Reskin shared enemy types per realm.** Hytale gives its undead a local look in each zone: plain, sand-swept, frost and burnt skeletons, plus undead cows and pigs in the fire zone. It also puts a fire-zone elite into a crypt in the starting zone as a surprise ([Devastated Lands](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Devastated_Lands), [Cursed Crypt](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Cursed_Crypt)).
16. **Boss crowd-control resistance, separate from player tenacity.** MnS caps how much chill can slow a mob by rarity, from 100% on common mobs to 25% on legendary mobs and bosses, and gives its strongest bosses a crowd-control-resist spell (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_mob_rarity). See SP-12.

### Avoid

17. **Flying bosses and flying phases.** Endgame & QoL's frost dragon opens with a flying phase that is immune to melee ([Bosses & Elites](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/bosses-elites)). Many AoA bosses fly: Shadowlord, Cotton Candor, Gyro, Graw, Craexxeus (AoA 1.12.2 source). The vision allows zero flying bosses.
18. **Immunities without a tell, unkillable mobs, item theft.** AoA's Mechamaton and Paladin ignore melee and ranged damage, and Tortione also ignores guns. The Destructor has 999 HP and is "completely unkillable by normal means", and the Quickpocket steals items (AoA 1.12.2 source; [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/AdventOfAscension)).
19. **Fights gated by skills.** AoA's Hunter skill made 71 mob types impossible to damage below a skill level (as high as 97). Ancient Cavern's bosses required level 70 in an unrelated skill plus a full favour bar ([Removed features/Hunter (skill)](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Hunter_(skill)); AoA 1.12.2 source, AncientCavernShrine).
20. **Stacked, opaque multipliers.** CTE2 multiplies mob power by linear and exponential level factors, rarity, per-mob config, dimension, map tier and level gap. The exponential damage factor alone grows from ×2.5 at level 1 to ×11.1 at level 100 (computed). The author reworked it to cut "gotcha moments" and cut damage another 12–25% in 2.1.0 (CTE2 changelog). Keep mob power a short, testable formula: level curve × rarity × optional override.
21. **Rare spawns and portable summons as boss access.** A CTE2 quest boss had a 5% chance to spawn when another mob died. AoA's Crystevia key needs the Case Construct, ≈1% of spawn weight. CTE2's summoning portals call bosses anywhere, which strips them of their place (CTE2 quests; AoA spawn registry).
22. **AI that only rushes.** A Hytale reviewer: enemies "don't currently use any strategy besides running straight at you" ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/impressions/hytale-early-access-impressions/)).

## 4. Level and chapter progression (PR): pace by time, gate by guaranteed items

CTE2 is a ladder of dimensions. Each act covers one or two dimensions, prints its level band in the quest book (Act I "Lv. 1-15", Act II "Lv. 10-30" and so on), and ends its main chain with a boss whose drop opens the next portal (CTE2 quests). The structure works. The pacing does not: the XP curve pushes about 89% of all XP into levels 50–100, the epilogue jumps from level 75 to 90, and the author retuned XP more than ten times. Hytale's progression runs on soft gates (ore tiers, bench upgrades, bestiary milestones) and was criticised as vague. AoA derives difficulty from its unlock graph.

### Adopt

1. **A chapter is a level band, its realm(s) and a short main chain.** CTE2's acts have 6–9 main quests each, about 36 across five acts out of ~620 quests in total. Each chain ends in a boss or item that opens the next dimension, for example the Wither's Nether Star crafted into the Undergarden portal catalyst (CTE2 quests). Everything else is optional.
2. **Quest types and teaching spread over time.** CTE2 teaches its quest shapes (main, side, repeatable, miniboss, shop, achievement) and spreads 22 click-to-read "lost pages" through the acts, so each concept arrives when it matters. A guide book repeats the text (CTE2 quests; CTE2 Patchouli book). Emit the quest and the guide entry from one YAML source ([ADR 0006](../decisions/0006-quests-ftb-quests-generated-from-data.md)).
3. **Milestone points through a rewards chapter that completes itself.** CTE2 grants 25 talent points across the acts, 10 spell points for boss trophies and 9 ascendancy points through quests that complete automatically (a `stat` task plus a dependency; CTE2 quests, rewards chapter). Use this for chapter-milestone bonus points.
4. **Repeatables in their own chapter.** CTE2 moved its repeatable quests out of the main line "to prevent players from getting 'stuck'" (CTE2 changelog 0.9.1). Each one unlocks with the quest that introduced its mob.
5. **Side content pays flat XP scaled by level.** CTE2 pays the player's level × 50 XP for a discovery, × 100 for a typical side quest, × 500 for a challenge and × 750 for a chapter achievement. Percentage rewards were rejected: "not a percentage, as it would be too strong at higher levels" (CTE2 changelog 0.4.0).
6. **A level-gap penalty instead of hard locks.** See BM-14. It lets the first realm open at level 1 and still be recommended from 5.
7. **Soft death.** CTE2 keeps the inventory and never takes gear; dying costs XP (5% of the current level) only from level 50 (CTE2 config and game rules).
8. **Something new to do in every chapter.** MnS unlocks every class spell by level 30, in rows at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30, so levels 30–100 only raise numbers (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_spell_school). With 10-level chapters, each chapter should bring spell slots (the 4·N rule), rank thresholds, a realm or a station tier.
9. **Visible direction.** A Hytale review: "Progression can sometimes be vague, requiring external research in forums and wikis" ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/impressions/hytale-early-access-impressions/)). Hytale answers with a chapter screen of objectives in 0.7.0 ([First Look](https://hytale.com/news/2026/7/first-look-chapter-1-and-more)). The RPG Leveling mod colours a zone green, orange or red by how well it matches the player's level ([RPG Leveling](https://api.cfwidget.com/hytale/mods/rpg-leveling-and-stats)). Show that colour on keys, portals and the realm map.

### Adapt

10. **Derive the XP curve from a time target, not a formula.** In MnS, XP for the next level is (10 · (1 + 0.2 L))^2.4, which rises from 389 at level 1 to 365,891 at level 99. A same-level mob's XP only rises from ~55 to ~178, so kills per level climb from 7 to ~2,055, and levels 50–100 hold ~89% of all XP (MnS source, LevelUtils; computed). Between 0.1.0 and 1.1.0, CTE2 retuned kill or quest XP more than ten times (CTE2 changelog). Instead, set a target number of minutes per level for each chapter and let quests supply a fixed share. In CTE2, campaign quests pay ≈1.36 M XP, about the base XP needed to reach level 52. Check it in the balance simulation.
11. **Teach through play.** CTE2's prologue is 25 linear quests, 23 of them click-to-read pages that cover ~20 systems before the first real fight. The pack later added more pages and fixed tooltips (CTE2 quests, prologue; CTE2 changelog). Teach one system per quest by doing it ("cast your spell on 5 statues"), reach the first fight within minutes, and leave the rest to lost pages.
12. **Exploration checklists per realm.** CTE2 has ~30 "discover structure X" quests and biome checklists (53 Overworld biomes), each paying the player's level × 50 XP (CTE2 quests, exploration). Locators must be honest (see PR-17).
13. **A bestiary for flavour, not gates.** Hytale's bestiary records each creature with the biome and time it was met, and pays out at 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 entries: decorative chests, teleporter slots, transformation potions, the portal block, backpack upgrades ([Memories](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Memories)). Use the loop for cosmetics, recipes and storage, never for waystones or travel.
14. **Gates are guaranteed boss items.** Hytale gates the alchemy bench's tiers on temple-golem gems. Endgame & QoL's dragon drops its heart at 100%, and the heart upgrades the bench that crafts the next key ([Alchemist's Workbench](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Alchemist%27s_Workbench), [Frozen Dungeon](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/frozen-dungeon)). Chapter gates should look like this.
15. **Place side realms with a tier rule.** AoA: "A dimension's tier is one more than that of the highest tiered dimension that must be visited to reach it" ([Dimensional Tiers](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Dimensional_Tiers)). Use this to slot optional realms into a chapter.

### Avoid

16. **Gaps between level bands.** CTE2's epilogue jumps to level 90 after Act V's 60–75, with only maps in between. A player asked: "why is the epilogue 30 levels further then the previous chapter?" ([review 4142](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/4142)).
17. **Quest targets that are hard or impossible to find.** CTE2 quest bosses live in structures that "cannot be found conventionally with the Explorer's Compass", or spawn with a 5% chance when another mob dies. The pack's fixes were all workarounds: pictures of the structures, key shops, summoning portals (CTE2 quests and changelog). Every target needs a locator, a summon item or a vendor fallback.
18. **Many separate point pools.** An MnS character manages stat, talent, spell, passive and ascendancy points, each with its own minor and major reset potion: ten respec potions (MnS 6.3.14 lang; CTE2 1.1.3 datapack). Keep Matěj's two pools, skill points and stat points.
19. **Parallel skill grinds as gates.** AoA 3.x ran 12 skills to level 100, each on 50 × 1.1^level XP. Reaching 100 took ≈3,600 orbs for Butchery or ≈44,000 movement ticks for Expedition (AoA 1.12.2 source; computed). The 3.6 rewrite "Rebalanced skill xp rates to be much more palatable" ([3.6 Public Alpha](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/blob/1.16.5/changelogs/3.6-Public%20Alpha.md)).
20. **Three party systems.** CTE2's prologue teaches three separate team systems: MnS teams (XP and healing), FTB Teams (quests) and Lightman's teams (money) (CTE2 quests, prologue). Build one party concept with quest sharing included.

## 5. Spells and skills (SP): few spells with ranks, not many spells with reskins

MnS defines **235 spells**. **170** of them belong to 10 classes (12–21 per class), and a player may invest in at most 2 classes. Spells go on an **8-slot bar** with up to 5 support gems each, rank up to 16 (+8 from gear), and stand beside **28 aura gems** that reserve a pool of 100 "spirit" (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack; MnS source). A build uses 6–8 of them; the rest is menu noise. Matěj's plan already answers this with an always-visible 8-slot bar, one starting spell per class, the 4·N cap and ranks with threshold effects; the sources add detail on ranks, toggles, the global cooldown and tenacity.

### Adopt

1. **The bar stays visible, even when empty.** CTE2 hides the spell bar while it holds no spell (`hide_spell_hotbar_when_no_spell = true`; CTE2 client config), which hides the system from new players. Matěj's always-visible bar is right.
2. **Single keys, no modifier chords.** MnS uses R, V, C, G and Shift+R/V/C/G by default. Its wiki advises that the last four "should be long cooldown spells, buffs or other things you rarely cast" (MnS wiki, how to cast spells). Keep Matěj's R T F G C V X Y layout and put rarely cast spells on the far keys.
3. **Clear cast-failure messages and a searchable spell library.** MnS tells the player why a cast failed (wrong weapon, no resource, on cooldown, already casting, not allowed here) and has a searchable in-game library of spells and unique items (MnS wiki). Add those reasons to C3b's tooltips and cast bar.
4. **Trade-offs at thresholds.** MnS's depth comes from drawbacks: Twincast adds a projectile but deals 20% LESS projectile damage; Blood Mage pays for spells with health instead of mana; Summoner gives +3 summons but 25% LESS of the player's own damage. Support gems pair MORE with LESS in the same way (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_perk, mmorpg_support_gem). Use this pattern for rank threshold effects and talents.
5. **Combo tags.** The Brawler class tags five spells each as linker, extender and finisher (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_spells). It is a cheap structure for the melee class.
6. **Cheap respec.** MnS has a minor reset (10 points) and a major one (all points). Its wiki says "Reset Potions are cheap, you should be using all your points every time you level up" ([MnS wiki, mistakes to avoid](https://moddedmc.wiki/en/project/mine-and-slash/latest/docs/newbie_guide/mistakes_to_avoid)). This matches the roadmap's respec command.
7. **Passive abilities as data, unlocked by level, each with a toggle.** AoA 1.21 defines each skill in JSON, with abilities at set levels, and players can switch each ability off. For example, Innervation gives +0.3 max health per level, halves the duration of debuffs at level 19 and adds +10 health at levels 25, 50 and 85 (AoA 1.21 data, player skills). Use this for passive and profession skills.
8. **Combat effects on their own time scale and data type.** CTE2's 137 combat effects last 3–30 s (most 4–10 s) and never share a slot with consumables that last 30–60 min (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_exile_effect, mmorpg_stat_effect).

### Adapt

9. **Ranks 1–8: rising numbers plus fixed thresholds.** MnS spreads a spell's values over 16 ranks: Fireball goes from 211.75% to 302.5% of weapon damage while its mana cost rises from 6 to 8 (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, fireball). CTE2 2.1 added effects at thresholds: fire skills change at 4 stacks of a heat buff, and support slots open at ranks 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 (CTE2 changelog 2.1.0). For 8 ranks: raise numbers every rank, add new effects at fixed ranks (e.g. 3, 5 and 8), and show the next threshold in the spellbook. For comparison, MnS's 110 spell points against 16 ranks per spell max out only 6–7 spells (computed).
10. **Toggles reserve resource; they do not pay per cast.** MnS auras each reserve 25–40 points of a 100-point pool, in up to 9 slots, and over-reserving switches them all off (MnS source, GemInventoryHelper). For Realmforge toggles: reserve a share of max mana, allow 2–3 at once, and keep them on the 8-slot bar. If a toggle drains instead, AoA's placeable aura blocks show the pace: a strong one empties a 200-point pool in ~25–30 s (AoA 1.12.2 source; computed).
11. **A global cooldown of about 1 s, with buffs, toggles and movement exempt.** CTE2 2.1.0 replaced per-spell cooldowns with a 1 s global "Recovery". Two days later the author wrote that "at high Skill Speed casting stuff feels fine, but it's when you're not there yet that it feels bad", and moved utility, buffs, curses and movement to a 0.25 s minimum instead (CTE2 changelog 2.1.0, 2.1.2). Start C3a with that split.
12. **Tenacity fills a real gap.** MnS has a resistance per ailment (100% means immunity) and a stat for "effectiveness of negative buffs on you", but nothing that shortens incoming effects (MnS 6.3.14 lang; MnS source, EffectStats). Give tenacity a cap (50–60%) and diminishing returns. AoA 1.21's ability to halve debuff duration, at Innervation 19, is a precedent for a passive version (AoA 1.21 data).
13. **Talents: few nodes, each with impact.** MnS's shared tree has 1,321 nodes. 950 of them are +attribute or +x% filler, including 70 copies of +1 INT, and a max-level character can take ~126 (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, talents; computed). M1's 8–10 nodes per class tree is the right scale.
14. **One or two effects per attribute.** One point of MnS STR gives +2.5 health, +0.2 regeneration, +0.25% armour, +0.1% damage, +0.25% crit damage and +1% summon health. Attributes on gear scale with level up to ×5.95, which dwarfs the points a player allocates (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_stat and game balance). Keep each Realmforge stat to 1–2 clear effects, so allocated points stay meaningful.
15. **Procs get internal cooldowns.** CTE2 gave proc skills a built-in 10-tick cooldown because they were "just too overwhelming in terms of combat power" (CTE2 changelog).

### Avoid

16. **Spell bloat.** 10 classes with 12–21 spells each (170) plus 8 passives each. Many are elemental reskins: three novas, four traps, eight totem-type spells in one class, three golems, three elemental finishers, and eight aura spells in one class next to 28 aura gems. All of them unlock by level 30, while a build uses 6–8 (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_spell_school). Give each class few spells with distinct roles, and add depth through ranks.
17. **Extra layers on top of spells.** 83 support gems and a separate layer of 28 aura gems. A reviewer: "Many support gem are not even useful" ([review 566](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/566)).
18. **Spells that scale from weapon damage.** MnS spell damage is mostly a percentage of weapon damage. A staff without MnS stats fails the cast ("Weapon has no Mine and Slash Stats/Soul"), so mages chase weapon damage like warriors (MnS wiki, how to cast spells). Give casters a spell-power stat.
19. **Stat sprawl.** MnS names 743 stats, CTE2's data uses 654, and 88 appear on gear prefixes and suffixes alone. The CTE2 author lists "confusion from certain skills and stats" among his main pain points (MnS 6.3.14 lang; CTE2 changelog 2.0.0). Aim for 30–40 stats the player sees.

## 6. Professions (PF): one tier per chapter, valves against grind, no XP sinks

CTE2 runs four gathering professions (mining, farming, fishing, bounty hunting), four crafting ones (cooking, alchemy, gear crafting, infusing) and salvaging. All level from 1 to 100, cannot pass the character's level, and switch material tier every 20 levels, tied to where each ore can be found (CTE2 quests, professions). From 2023 to 2026 the author kept making professions cheaper and better connected, and the pack itself admits "Mine and Slash Professions can sometimes be tedious to level" (CTE2 quests, Act II). AoA 3.x skills were longer parallel grinds and hard gates. Hytale ties its bench tiers to boss gems and plans fewer benches.

### Adopt

1. **Profession level ≤ character level, and one material tier per chapter.** CTE2 changes tier every 20 levels; Realmforge changes it every 10. Chapter 1 uses Overworld and stone-realm materials, chapter 2 desert and tundra materials (CTE2 quests, professions; MnS source).
2. **Deterministic recipes generated from one definition.** CTE2's 810 recipe files (tier × rarity × recipe family) are deterministic: the rarity stone put in decides the output's rarity. They are also copied by hand, and bugs shipped, such as "level 80-99 jewelry profession recipes containing air" (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack; CTE2 changelog 1.0.6). Generate the tiers from one YAML entry with a tier parameter and unit-test the formulas.
3. **Stations that craft in bulk and read nearby chests.** MnS stations repeat the locked recipe while materials last (MnS source, ProfessionBlockEntity). Hytale benches pull from chests up to 14 blocks away horizontally and 6 vertically ([Update 1](https://hytale.com/news/2026/1/hytale-patch-notes-update-1)). CTE2 turned chest-reading off; turn it on.
4. **Valves against grind.** Rested profession XP: each point of combat XP adds 0.25 to a pool that doubles profession XP until it is spent, and profession XP feeds combat at 0.05 (CTE2 config; MnS source, RestedExpData). A shop paid in contract currency sells other professions' materials, gated by profession level (CTE2 profession shop). Both fit M7 contracts.
5. **Mining as the M1 profession.** XP per ore by tier (CTE2: coal 10, iron 15, gold 20, diamond 30, the top tier 80), a 20% base chance of a realm crafting material scaled by the ore's XP, and new tool affixes as the pickaxe levels (CTE2: one every 20 levels) (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_profession). Add AoA's treasure ore: the Deep Case, a stone block with its own loot table that respects Fortune (AoA 1.12.2 source, DeepCase).
6. **Ambient creatures to harvest.** AoA's pixons are glowing creatures that players drain with a bowl for realm-specific infusion stones, without killing them (AoA 1.12.2 bestiary). One feature adds life and a reason to roam.

### Adapt

7. **Quality from realm materials, in three steps.** CTE2 sets crafted rarity with stones salvaged from gear, which ties crafting to the gear-loot loop, across six rarities (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack). Let a realm material (such as a gem dust) set quality, in about three steps, so a level-1 player can craft from Overworld and stone-realm drops alone.
8. **A pace target per chapter.** In CTE2, crafting takes ≈4–12 crafts per level (computed: a common recipe gives 100 XP at skill 1 and 750 at skill 10). Gathering takes ~21 iron ores per level at level 1 and ~103 at level 19 (computed from MnS formulas). Target ~5–10 crafts and a few dozen gathering actions per level, so a chapter's profession levels take about as long as the chapter. With 10-level chapters, a tool affix every 5 levels.
9. **Key recipes that span several activities.** Endgame & QoL's swamp key needs an elite's drop, a mined gem, a smelted ingot, a second elite's drop and a crafted rope ([Swamp Dungeon](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/swamp-dungeon)). Use three to five ingredients from different realm activities, drop elite ingredients at 100%, and show sources in-game through EMI and Jade.
10. **Station tiers unlocked by guaranteed boss items (PR-14), and fewer benches.** Hytale plans to reduce "the number of benches", moving work "into in-world activities like wood chopping and metalworking" ([First Look](https://hytale.com/news/2026/7/first-look-chapter-1-and-more)).
11. **A rotating boosted material.** MnS has a message for a daily rotation of boosted profession drops, but the code is disabled (MnS 6.3.14 lang; MnS source). A rotation per chapter or per session varies gathering without real-time chores.
12. **Bounty boards later, without dailies.** CTE2 levels its bounty-hunting profession through a board that rerolls every 45 s; Endgame & QoL gives 3 daily bounties and 1 weekly, with reputation ranks (CTE2 config; [Bounty Board](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/bounty-board)). In a mostly solo game, contracts should refresh when completed, not on a clock.

### Avoid

13. **Walls of gathering late in a tier.** More than 100 actions per level near the end of a CTE2 tier; the pack itself calls professions "tedious to level" (CTE2 quests, Act II).
14. **Four inputs per craft.** A CTE2 recipe takes a tier material, a rarity stone, a flavour item and a coin in one of four metals. Early consumables should need 2–3 inputs.
15. **XP sinks.** A "Destroy Output" item voids the crafted item for double XP, or triple per the 6.3.14 text (MnS source; MnS 6.3.14 lang).
16. **Skills as permissions.** AoA gated each infusion creature and each enchantment recipe on Infusion level, and some bosses on level 70 in other skills. The rewrite switched to perks that players can toggle (AoA 1.12.2 source; [3.6 Public Alpha](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/blob/1.16.5/changelogs/3.6-Public%20Alpha.md)).
17. **Chains with a single source.** Hytale's teleporters need a wood from one forest biome plus a mid-game bench ([Teleporter](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Teleporter)).

## 7. Food and potions (FP): CTE2's slots and magnitudes, Hytale's food tiers, much longer timers

Matěj's rule (weak, long-lasting, craftable early) matches CTE2 almost exactly and runs against Hytale and AoA. CTE2 has three buff slots that last 30–60 minutes, and instant potions that restore a percentage of the maximum. Hytale's cooked food lasts at most 6 minutes ([Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)). AoA's foods give bursts of 2–10 seconds, and AoA never implemented potions.

### Adopt

1. **Three buff slots, one active buff each.** CTE2 has an Elixir slot (alchemy, 30 min), a Meal slot (cooking, 60 min) and a Seafood slot (cooking, 60 min). A second meal replaces the first, but buffs in different slots stack, and each shows a status icon with the time left: "You can have One Consumable active per each type" (MnS source, PlayerBuffData; MnS 6.3.14 lang).
2. **Baseline strength.** CTE2's simplest life meal gives +5.5% max health and +6.8% health regeneration for 60 min, and the simplest seafood +3.6% XP. The simplest elixirs give +5.9% STR or +5.4% physical damage for 30 min (computed from the CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_stat_buff). That is Matěj's "+5% damage for an hour". Add ~1–2 percentage points per chapter instead of CTE2's six rarities.
3. **Instant potions restore a percentage of the maximum, on a shared cooldown.** CTE2 health and mana potions restore 5 + 0.25 × rarity maximum × tier multiplier percent (computed: 9.2% for the common first tier, 30% for the mythic first tier, 67.5% for the mythic top tier). One craft yields 4 potions, and all potions of a type share a cooldown. CTE2 cut that cooldown from 30 s to 10 s "to encourage players (especially early on) to craft and use these" (MnS source, SlashPotionItem; CTE2 changelog 2.0.0).
4. **A quick-drink key that picks the best potion the player may use.** Since CTE2 2.0.0 the key drinks "the best potion within your level range" (CTE2 changelog 2.0.0). CTE2's default key is Y, which is one of Realmforge's spell keys, so pick another default.
5. **Foods tagged by flavour category, one stat per category.** CTE2 tags 479 foods (823 tag entries) into 14 categories: grain → max health, vegetable → mana, meat → health regeneration, spice → fire damage, alcohol → XP, and so on. Within a category, strength grows with the dish's complexity: raw, cooked, composed (CTE2 datapack, food tags; [MetaMods](https://metamods.net/en/mods/mine-and-meals)). This is the tool for rebalancing Farmer's Delight. Publish the values in tooltips; the mod CTE2 uses hides them.
6. **Hytale's food families, CTE2's durations.** Hytale's food comes in three tiers: raw (45 s), simple (90 s) and feast (6 min). They give +5/10/15% max health, +20/30% stamina, or regeneration ([Entity Effect](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Entity_Effect), [Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)). Keep the families and the raw → simple → feast ladder, stretched to 20–60 min.

### Adapt

7. **Brewing without the Nether.** CTE2 left vanilla brewing alone, crafted its RPG potions instantly at an alchemy station, and dropped Nether Wart from healing potions early (CTE2 changelog 0.2.1). With the Nether and End removed, define potion bases and fire reagents from Overworld and stone-realm materials, and decide whether brewing keeps a timed process (open question 8).
8. **Food variety rewards only with explicit stacking rules.** CTE2 rewards variety among the last 48 foods (newer ones weigh more) with milestones from +2 to +60 max health. Its Spice of Life version merges same-attribute rewards by maximum, so the repeated +1 milestones probably never add up, and one crafted meal counts as 10 variety points against bread's 1 (CTE2 config; [Spice of Life source](https://github.com/CreativeMD/Spice-of-Life-Onion/tree/1.20); computed). If used: additive milestones in data, a food-book screen, crafted buff meals weighted separately.
9. **Utility potions as realm counters.** Hytale's antidote protects against spider poison for 2 minutes ([Update 4](https://hytale.com/news/2026/3/hytale-patch-notes-update-4)). Long-lasting resistance potions (heat, cold, pressure) can be the counters for RE-6.
10. **Delayed healing as a skill element.** Hytale health potions heal part of the amount at once and the rest after 5 s, and the delayed part "is removed when taking damage" ([Health Potion](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Health_Potion)). This is an option for a higher potion tier.
11. **A daily favour buff only if it is long and tied to a realm.** AoA's tribute filled four bars through daytime actions to earn a night-long buff in the Overworld. It was removed as "arbitrary", "hard to balance" and redundant with potions ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)).

### Avoid

12. **Short buff windows.** AoA's food buffs last 2–10 s (one tea gives Regeneration IV for 3.75 s); Hytale's cooked food lasts 6 min (AoA 1.12.2 source; [Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)).
13. **Strong consumables, or consumables tied to endgame loot.** CTE2 set its seafood damage bonus at 2–10%, against Mine and Slash's default 5–25%, and still had to shorten potion cooldowns in 2.0.0 before players used them (CTE2 datapack against the MnS jar; CTE2 changelog 2.0.0).
14. **Undefined stacking and unclamped formulas.** MnS adds the rarity percentage and the item level without a cap, so high tiers exceed the stated maximum (MnS source, StatBuff).
15. **Level requirements that fail silently.** CTE2 shipped potions whose level requirements were not checked (fixed in 2.0.0), and foods above the player's level fail with "[Food Eat Fail]" (CTE2 changelog; MnS 6.3.14 lang). Show requirements in tooltips.

## 8. Loot and economy (LE): guaranteed chain items, one currency, bounded crafting

MnS loot is plentiful and random. Every eligible kill has a 10% chance of gear, across 6 rarities plus 220 uniques and 57 runewords, and crafting uses 42 currency items, several of which can destroy the item (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack and config). Players call the result "the tedious grind 1000 gear for single useful piece" ([review 566](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/566)). AoA ran a separate token currency per dimension, then folded them into coins. The Hytale mods show the safe pattern for progression items.

### Adopt

1. **Chain items drop at 100%.** Major Dungeons' first treasure bag holds the next key at 100%, and a dungeon coin at 70% ([Treasure Bag DoF I](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/treasure-bag-dungeon-of-fear-i)). Endgame & QoL's dragon heart drops at 100% ([Frozen Dungeon](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/frozen-dungeon)).
2. **A deterministic fallback for every key.** Major Dungeons puts a vendor in every treasure room who sells all keys for dungeon coins, its workaround for having to start over after a failed boss ([Nameless God](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/nameless-god), [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/major-dungeons)). CTE2's quest NPC sells keys "Should you find a ... Dungeon that's already been cleared" (CTE2 quests, Act IV). The stone realm's mine NPCs, who trade the first keys, should sell later keys too.
3. **One currency for the whole game.** AoA ran a coin ladder (bankers exchange 20:1, so 1 Lunaver = 8,000 copper) beside 21 per-dimension tokens spent at identical lottery vendors. In 3.5 it removed the tokens "in favor of using coins as a universal currency" ([Removed features/Tokens](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Removed_features/Tokens), [3.5 Beta 5](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/blob/1.16.5/changelogs/3.5-Beta-5.md)). Use realm materials as secondary costs.
4. **Affix rolls banded by rarity.** In MnS an affix's rarity fixes its roll band: common rolls 0–17% of the stat's range, uncommon 18–34%, and so on up to mythic at 86–100% (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, mmorpg_gear_rarity). Five 20% bands give M2's five tiers a quality the player can read.
5. **Bounded crafting, with no destruction.** Every MnS item has a budget of 75–200 "potential", and each crafting currency spends 0–25 of it, which caps rerolling (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, library_of_exile_currency). Keep a budget (for example a material cost that rises with each reroll), without the outcomes that destroy the item 25% of the time.
6. **Rolls per player.** Boss rewards roll for every player who dealt damage and drop as items owned by that player, and co-op kills roll loot for each player separately ([MD Bosses](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/bosses), [E&QoL Introduction](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/introduction)). CTE2 gives each player their own chest contents (the Lootr mod). Build this now, so the party system needs no redesign.
7. **A fixed damage step per gear tier.** Endgame & QoL rebalanced its weapons to "a clean +30% damage step at every tier" ([cfwidget](https://api.cfwidget.com/hytale/mods/endgame-qol)). A simple anchor for gear by chapter.
8. **Upgrade kits keep early weapons useful.** AoA upgrades a weapon with a realm-themed kit at a station; it has 18 kits (AoA 1.12.2 lang). Make the station available early: AoA placed it only in its tier-4 dimension (AoA 1.12.2 source, Shyrelands generator).
9. **Few drops, with auto-salvage and filters from day one.** CTE2's 10% gear roll per kill, plus nine other drop tables, forced a loot bag, automatic salvaging and a filter by item type. The filter only arrived in 2.1.0, as a "long-requested feature" (CTE2 config; CTE2 changelog 2.1.0).

### Adapt

10. **Traders as a reason to return.** AoA's useful traders sold boss summon items, portal parts and realm gear; Celeve's toy merchant sells the boss item for 15 circus coins (AoA 1.12.2 source, trader classes). Restock per chapter or on demand, not on real-time days. Endgame & QoL restocks daily and Hytale's tree-folk merchant every 3 in-game days ([Frozen Dungeon](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/frozen-dungeon), [Merchant](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Merchant)).
11. **Choice instead of rolls.** AoA's lottery totem lets the player pick one of several items ("Choose wisely!"), and CTE2 quests offer pick-one reward tables (AoA 1.12.2 lang; CTE2 quests). Use deterministic choice rewards for chapter milestones.
12. **Harder modes pay through better tables.** Major Dungeons' Hardcore bag doubles the chance of the boss weapon (10% → 20%) and raises the coin chance from 70% to 80% ([Treasure Bag DoF I (Hardcore)](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/treasure-bag-dungeon-of-fear-i-hardcore)). See DU-14.
13. **Anti-farm rules only if mild and visible.** CTE2 gives 15 free kills per chunk, then cuts loot by 2% per kill, recovers 5% per minute and adds 5 free kills per looted chest; instances and bosses are exempt (CTE2 config, anti_mob_farm). A reviewer: "removing all mob drops is kinda dumb" ([review 521](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/521)). If used, show it in the HUD (open question 9).

### Avoid

14. **Stacks of meta-currencies.** CTE2 chains maps, map affixes, curse coins, relics, boss fragments and a map passive tree, plus separate gems and essences for its side arenas, each retuned repeatedly (CTE2 changelog). Major Dungeons adds collectible cards and booster packs; Endgame & QoL a combo meter (up to +100% damage with lifesteal), bounties, reputation and leaderboards ([Combo Meter](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/combo-meter), [Devil Merchant](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/devil-merchant)).
15. **Gear progress by luck.** "getting better gear felt like a chore at times since it mostly boils down to luck" ([review 4142](https://moddex.gg/modpack/craft-to-exile-2/reviews/4142)).
16. **Gambling with destruction.** One MnS currency destroys the item 25% of the time; harvest essences 15% (CTE2 1.1.3 datapack, library_of_exile_currency).
17. **Low-percentage chase drops.** Endgame & QoL's boss pets drop at 10–15% (30% in its config); Major Dungeons' wolf mount at ~1% ([cfwidget](https://api.cfwidget.com/hytale/mods/endgame-qol), [Silver Moon bag](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/major-dungeons/treasure-bag-silver-moon)). If a chase item exists, add a token vendor or bad-luck protection.
18. **Filler loot.** Hytale's loot tables, high-roll chests included, can hold sticks, plant fibre, crude arrows and weighted "No Loot Generated" entries ([Dungeon](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Dungeon)). AoA's Deeplands had no chests at all.
19. **Loot meters punished by death.** CTE2's Favor meter (up to 2,500, worth up to +25% loot) loses 500 on every death (CTE2 config).
20. **Too many weapon types and ammo kinds.** AoA 3.3.6 has 412 weapons in 13 classes, with bullets, cannonballs, shotgun shells, special arrows, 15 rune types and energy as ammunition (AoA 1.12.2 source, WeaponRegister).

## 9. World fullness (WF): budget life and landmarks per km², and measure it

AoA's numbers explain its emptiness. Every 1.12.2 dimension was a single biome. A point of interest appeared every **26–212 chunks**, and **15 of 22 dimensions** (13 of the 20 open-world ones) had no passive animal. All structures were placed block by block in Java code (AoA 1.12.2 source). The community summed up the loop as find the boss items, fight the boss, shop, move on ([#1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)). Hytale shows the opposite and its overshoot: the world is full of tiered settlements, factions and hints, but one reviewer could not find free land to build on. CTE2's fullness was borrowed from ~25 structure mods, so its spacing values make a usable calibration.

### Adopt

1. **A measured density budget per realm, in three layers.**
   - Small sites (camps, ruins, mine entrances): spacing ~12–16 chunks, separation 6–8. AoA 1.21 uses 12–14 / 6–8; a small Mowzie's Mobs chamber in CTE2 uses 10 / 6.
   - Medium sites: spacing 18–42 chunks.
   - Landmarks: spacing 40–80 chunks (CTE2: 40 for mansions, 80 / 42 for its rarest tower).

   Sources: AoA 1.21 worldgen data; CTE2 datapack, structure_set. For scale, one structure set at spacing 12 gives ~27 sites per km² (3,906 chunks ÷ 144; computed). AoA's Deeplands had ≈24 per km² from all its types together and felt empty; Dustopia, the densest of AoA's A–G dimensions, had ≈88. Add a GameTest that counts structures per N chunks: AoA 3.3.6 shipped three copy-paste placement bugs and two structures that never generate (AoA 1.12.2 source).
2. **Spawn tables per biome, with passive, ambient and hostile categories.** AoA 1.21 Precasia has 13 biomes with 27–36 placed features each. Grazers come in herds of 2–4 and predators in packs of 2–3; rivers have frogs and tadpoles, the ocean fish and squid, and caves their own species (AoA 1.21 worldgen data). This is the template for "as full as a modded Overworld".
3. **A faction pair per realm, with tiered settlements.** Hytale's zone design pairs a friendly or neutral faction with a hostile one, each with its own blocks, furniture and chests. In Early Access the pairs exist in Zone 1 (Kweebecs and Trorks, plus Goblins and Klops) and Zone 2 (Feran and Scaraks); Zone 3's Bramblekin and both Zone 4 factions are still planned only. Three-tier settlements exist for specific families: Trork camps (house and campfire → quarry or lumber camp → castle), Outlander villages and ruins ([Faction](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Faction), [Trork Camp](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Trork_Camp), [Outlander Village](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Outlander_Village)).
4. **Variants inside each structure family.** Optional rooms, alternative entrances, themed reskins: Hytale's Shale Dungeon has rooms that only some copies get, and its tree-folk villages come in three types ([Shale Dungeon](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Shale_Dungeon), [Kweebec Village](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Kweebec_Village)).
5. **Visual hints in the terrain.** Hytale's world generation design: "ash trees spawn above caves in the deeproot biome… players can learn that deeproot caves, which contain special resources, are found under dark ash trees on the map" ([Future of World Generation](https://hytale.com/news/2026/1/the-future-of-world-generation)). Ice spikes and a campfire mark a yeti cave, and graveyards mark crypts ([Yeti](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Yeti)). In Minecraft these are placed features with conditions.
6. **Inhabitants come back.** Hytale's golems respawn after one in-game day, and a cleared camp refills when the player returns ([Golem](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Golem), [Trork Camp](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Trork_Camp)). CTE2's structures regenerate after 96 h (DU-17). Persistent realms never stay cleared and empty.
7. **Lore in ruins and tooltips.** Hytale tells its fire zone's history through tooltips on ingots and cloth and through defaced statues ([Lore and Philosophy](https://hytale.com/news/2026/1/a-look-at-hytale-s-lore-and-philosophy)).
8. **A hub that grows with each chapter.** Each Hytale story chapter "will update the temple design" of its portal hub, which already has unfinished buildings for future features ([Forgotten Temple](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Forgotten_Temple_%28Edge_of_the_Echo%29)). The plaza around the portal structure should gain NPCs and services each chapter.
9. **Settlements next to danger.** AoA's liveliest places pair friendly NPCs with a boss site: Dustopia's village of five NPCs (1 per 200 chunks) beside the boss shrine, and Mysterium's village of friendly Gorb traders (AoA 1.12.2 source). The M1 mine with friendly NPCs near the boss arena follows this pattern.

### Adapt

10. **World events in persistent realms.** Hytale's goblin portals open at most every 30 min and vanish after 5 min if nobody comes near ([Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)). Endgame & QoL's random portals spawn about every 1.5 h, 80–300 blocks away, at most 3 at once, weighted 50/30/10 from exploration-only to hard ([Temporal Portals](https://wiki.hytalemodding.dev/mod/endgame-qol/temporal-portals)). For Realmforge: spawn near the player or a waystone, mark them on the map, let them last longer, and keep them optional and configurable.
11. **Events, not night-time swarms.** AoA's Overworld felt alive thanks to seven random daily events and mob families per biome (AoA 1.12.2 config), but so many event mobs slowed all spawning ([Player questions](https://adventofascension.fandom.com/wiki/Player_questions)). Hytale removed night-only Void mobs from the open world in 0.7.0 ([Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)).
12. **Sub-zones with their own water and fauna.** Hytale's oasis basins, tar pits, hot springs and glacier lakes each have fauna found nowhere else: flamingos only at oases, the yeti only on the highest peaks ([Oasis](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Oasis), [Yeti](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Yeti)).

### Avoid

13. **Sparse, purely random placement.** AoA 1.12 rolled each structure as a 1-in-N chance per chunk, so signature structures appeared once per ~300–1,700 chunks, and the Deeplands averaged one structure per 163 chunks (AoA 1.12.2 config; computed).
14. **Only hostile life.** AoA 1.12.2 registers 235 hostile spawn entries against 57 creature entries, most of them NPCs; 15 of 22 dimensions (13 of the 20 open-world ones) have no passive animal (AoA 1.12.2 source, EntitySpawnRegister).
15. **One cheap structure inflating the count.** Crystevia's high density (≈87 per km²) comes from one repeated converter hut (AoA 1.12.2 config).
16. **Landmarks with no reason to stop.** Vox Ponds' 97-block towers hold no chests and no mobs. Gardencia's castles appear once per 1,650–1,700 chunks, and players called it "one of the most empty dimensions" (AoA 1.12.2 source; [#1895](https://github.com/Tslat/Advent-Of-Ascension/issues/1895)).
17. **The same service set in every realm.** Almost every AoA dimension had a rune shrine, a lottery stall and a boss altar, built from one template (AoA 1.12.2 source).
18. **Too dense, or placeholders.** A Hytale reviewer: "Sometimes it's hard to find a blank stretch of land for building without being within arms reach of an infested tower or ancient ruin" ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/impressions/hytale-early-access-impressions/)). Others flagged work-in-progress signs and a vendor "who can't vend anything" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/hytales-developer-warned-us-the-adventure-sandbox-isnt-good-yet-but-it-already-feels-pretty-good-to-me/)).
19. **Structures written in code.** AoA 1.12.2 placed its structures block by block in Java: 432 classes, ~258,000 lines (AoA 1.12.2 source; line count). [ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-procedural-dungeons-and-dimensions.md)'s NBT rooms already avoid this.

## 10. Travel and QoL (TR): CTE2's cheap travel, Hytale's gadgets without the gates

CTE2 removes most walking. Waystones are plentiful and cost coins rather than XP, the glider needs no stamina, and starting kits come with warp scrolls. Real flight, though, waits until late in the game. Hytale makes comfort depend on long gates: crafted teleporter pairs need a wood from one biome, and their number is capped by bestiary milestones. Matěj's stance (easy waystones, early flight or a teleport spell, no long walks) sits closest to CTE2 with the flight moved early.

### Adopt

1. **Cheap waystones everywhere.** CTE2 generates waystones at frequency 20, forces them into villages and makes generated ones unbreakable. Travel costs coins (1 per 100 m, at most 10) rather than XP: the XP cost is set to 0, except 3 levels to change dimension. Since 2.0.2 every player gets a free warp stone in the prologue, and its cooldown fell from 6 to 2 minutes (CTE2 config; CTE2 changelog 2.0.2). Put a waystone at every realm hub and in every settlement tier.
2. **An emergency return.** CTE2's quest book provides a return scroll as its emergency item, on a 30 s cooldown (CTE2 quests, quest book settings).
3. **A glider without stamina, early.** CTE2 configured its glider with no stamina at all ("Completely removed Stamina from the game", 0.5.2) and made it unbreakable (CTE2 config).
4. **Party travel tools for later.** An item that teleports a player to a teammate (3 or 15 uses in CTE2; [9minecraft](https://www.9minecraft.net/telepass-mod/)), and a recall that takes every party member within 10 blocks to the boss room (CTE2 KubeJS, boss locator).
5. **Comfort from minute one.** A backpack, quick-stack to nearby chests (radius 4), a trash slot, sleeping bags, a vein miner (32 blocks), a magnet ("incredibly useful for dungeons") and a radial menu for books and screens (CTE2 config; CTE2 quests).
6. **Void and fall rescue in sky and void realms** (RE-15).

### Adapt

7. **Cross-realm travel through the portal hub.** CTE2 allows warps to another dimension only through global waystones (CTE2 config). Put a waystone inside the shared portal structure, so the hub is the node between realms.
8. **Early flight from a quest.** CTE2 rations flight: a flying mount breed arrives in the Nether chapter, the elytra after the Ender Dragon at level 50+ (CTE2 quests). Endgame & QoL's pet, fed from tier D up to SS, becomes a mount at tier C and flies at tier B ([cfwidget](https://api.cfwidget.com/hytale/mods/endgame-qol)). Make wings or a flying mount a guaranteed reward of a chapter 1–2 quest.
9. **Traversal gadgets.** Hytale's 0.7.0 glider dives and climbs on sprint (limited by stamina), rocket boots give a double jump, and a hookshot pulls the player to anchors they placed ([Scrap Glider](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Scrap_Glider), [Update 7 pre-release](https://hytale.com/news/2026/9/pre-release-patch-notes-update-7)). Good chapter-1 tools, if they are cheap.
10. **Taming mounts with their favourite food.** Hytale tames its horse, camel and ram by holding the food each likes, shown in a thought bubble ([Mount](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Mount)). One mount per realm style.
11. **Map markers for destinations.** Hytale always marks its hub portals and shows volcano treasure as a yellow spot on the map ([Forgotten Temple Gateway](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Forgotten_Temple_Gateway), [Volcano](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Volcano)). Mark dungeon portals and hubs once discovered, and never make a quest target that no locator finds (PR-17).

### Avoid

12. **Comfort behind long chains with one source.** Hytale's teleporters need a wood from one biome and a bench, come in pairs, and their cap grows with bestiary milestones (2 to 8 per the wiki; guides disagree). Its glider needs a rare bear hide ([Teleporter](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Teleporter), [Scrap Glider](https://hytalewiki.org/w/Scrap_Glider)).
13. **Long cooldowns and costs by distance.** CTE2's original 6-minute warp stone cooldown (now 2), and its predecessor's "Global waystones intrinsically cost more vanilla XP… and impose an increased cool down" ([ModDex: Dissonance](https://moddex.gg/modpack/crafttoexiledissonance)).
14. **Flight only at the end.** CTE2's elytra arrives at level 50+ (CTE2 quests).
15. **Glue scripts for core travel.** CTE2 relies on KubeJS scripts for the return from dungeons and for finding the boss, with a hard-coded fallback position (1802, 66, -423) (CTE2 KubeJS). Build these into the core mod (DU-5).

## Conclusion

All three reference games failed where they spent their effort, not through their mechanics. AoA spent it on 412 weapons and 35 bosses rather than animals, sound and mid-sized places; CTE2 on systems rather than a pacing target, then on three years of trimming them; Hytale on a world before reasons to stay in it. Each has since moved toward the same fixes: AoA's rebuilt realms are multi-biome and full of passive life, CTE2 2.0 cut consumable power and potion cooldowns and moved maps from level 55 to 50, and Hytale's chapters bring objectives and its first real dungeon. Realmforge can start where they ended up. Its specific risk is the reverse of AoA's: agents produce systems cheaply, while a realm full of life costs content time and review, so the budget must be measurable: a density GameTest, spawn categories per biome, minutes-per-level targets, a source check for every progression item.

Three rules decide most of the calls above. **Every progression item has a guaranteed source** (100% chain drops plus a vendor fallback), which keeps RNG grind off the path that matters. **A realm is judged by what the player meets per minute, not by its size**, which turns "full like a modded Overworld" into numbers an agent can test. **One engine per concern**: one encounter schema for phases, waves and boss chains, one currency, one party system, one buff-slot model. Every reference game that grew a second engine for the same job (AoA's 21 token economies, CTE2's six map currencies, Major Dungeons' card economy) later had to cut or patch it.

## Open questions for Matěj

1. **Respawn in realms (RE-16).** Where does a player who dies in a realm respawn: at the realm's hub camp, at the portal structure, or at their Overworld bed?
2. **Finished realms (RE-4).** Should a realm get a harder "enraged" band once later chapters raise the cap, or stay at its own levels?
3. **Death in an instance (DU-14, LE-12).** When a solo player dies in an instance, does the run end with the key lost, end with the key refunded, or does the player respawn at the instance entrance?
4. **Key modifiers and difficulty modes (DU-13, DU-14).** Wanted at all? If so, from which chapter?
5. **Wave instances (DU-8, DU-9).** With or without a timer? Does death fail the run, or does it pay out for the waves cleared so far?
6. **Respawning open-world dungeons (DU-17).** After a fixed time (CTE2: 96 h), or once the player leaves and comes back (Hytale)?
7. **World events (WF-10).** Should portals or rifts open inside persistent realms (now, later, never), and how often?
8. **Brewing (FP-7).** Keep a timed brewing process, or craft potions instantly at an alchemy station?
9. **Anti-farm (LE-13).** Any diminishing returns per area at all?
10. **Food variety (FP-8).** Include a variety reward, and if so, with which rewards?
11. **Bestiary (PR-13).** Do you want a creature log, and what should it unlock?
12. **Default keys (SP-2, FP-4).** Vanilla Minecraft binds T to chat and F to swapping hands. Should the spell bar take those keys by default and move the vanilla bindings, or use other defaults? Which key drinks a potion?
13. **Party scaling (BM-13).** +25% (AoA) or +50% (Endgame & QoL) boss health per extra player?
14. **Tenacity cap (SP-12).** 50%, 60% or another value?
15. **Environmental rules (RE-6).** One per realm, such as heat in the desert and cold in the tundra? Hytale has no temperature system, while AoA gave many realms a rule with a counter item.
16. **Trader restocking (LE-10).** Per chapter, per in-game day, or on demand?
