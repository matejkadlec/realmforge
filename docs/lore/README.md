# Lore

The background story of Realmforge and the rules for every text a player reads. The story stays in the
background ([vision](../vision.md)): it gives quests, bosses and items a reason to exist and follows the
[design documents](../design/README.md), never the other way round. A lore document names its Jira ticket, lists
its open questions for Matěj, and changes when he decides something new.

| Document | What it covers | Ticket |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [outline.md](outline.md) | The story: Realmforge, the Void, the Wall and its seals, the Ithari and Skyhearth, the war, the player's arc and the finale, one section per realm, factions and recurring characters, chapters 1 and 2 in short | RLM-43, RLM-464 |
| [style.md](style.md) | Voice, text types and lengths (realm names with their subtitle included), fixed terms, name rules per people, English only, a checklist | RLM-43, RLM-464 |
| [practices.md](practices.md) | How to write story beats, chapter texts and quests: the rules behind Matěj's corrections to chapters 1 and 2, and a checklist before a chapter goes to him | RLM-413 |
| [chapters/01-the-ominous-tale.md](chapters/01-the-ominous-tale.md) | Chapter 1, The Ominous Tale (Overworld, levels 1–10): the approved full text, each part with its story and its quests | RLM-351, RLM-464 |
| [chapters/02-the-weight-of-stone.md](chapters/02-the-weight-of-stone.md) | Chapter 2, The Weight of Stone (Undervault, levels 6–18): the approved full text, each part with its story and its quests | RLM-351, RLM-464 |

Matěj approved the outline and the style guide on 2026-10-13 (RLM-139); the outline records his answers and his later
decisions. He approved chapter 1 on 2026-10-24 and chapter 2 on 2026-10-27 (RLM-351), and the outline and the style
guide follow them. Every session that writes story or quests reads the [practices](practices.md) first, then the
chapters and the outline; a quest's texts come from its chapter.
