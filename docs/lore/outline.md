## At a glance

- **Realmforge** is the world, and its name is its history: it was forged. Around it lies the **Void**, an endless
  nothing that wears away any world nobody holds. About 5,000 years ago the **Gatewrights**, the guild of makers of
  a long-lived people, the **Ithari**, stamped five **realms** into the Void around Overworld: stone, the twins sun
  and ice, the deep and the mire. Side by side they close Overworld's whole shore: **the Wall**.
- **Seals and keepers:** each realm's first gate was shut for good. That gate is its **seal**, and an Ithari
  **keeper** (a Sealbound) is bound into it and holds it. Keepers are relieved now and then, like a changing of the
  guard. Since the war, 700 years ago, reliefs have been rare, the oldest keepers are tiring, and their seals crack.
- **The Chain of Seals,** which the Ithari call the road: the Ithari pass every gate, but the trials stand aside for
  them, so they never grow stronger. For someone who can, the Gatewrights left one **signet** in the Lodge's hall, and
  behind every seal a **guardian**, a trial that holds the **shards** of the next signets. Every signet opens only its
  own seal; there is no master signet. That is why the realms open one by one, in order.
- **The player** is a human whom the **Wayfarers' Lodge**, the Ithari's order of road-wardens, calls a **Wayfarer**.
  Only a human grows stronger with every trial passed, and a fallen human is pulled home to Overworld.
- **Maren Vell**, the Lodge's archivist, lives in **Skyhearth**, the Gatewrights' floating city, and reaches the
  player through **Skyhearth's Mirror** as **Maren's Image**.
- **The finale** waits on Unmade Isles: its final boss is **Elenyor, the Unforger**, whom history calls the First
  Gatewright, the Gatewrights' founder (Matěj, RLM-139, RLM-357). The player learns who he is only there.
- **Every realm has two peoples:** a friendly one to trade and quest with, and a hostile one to fight (WF-3), plus one
  element that shows on its mobs and drops (RE-5).
- **The tone is adventure, not dread:** an old, slightly mysterious world, told in ruins, inscriptions, murals and
  short quest texts (WF-7). No horror, no realm without light (RE-18).

## The world

### Realmforge and the Void

The Void is an endless nothing, and the worlds float in it like islands in a sea. It wears away any world that
nobody holds, and it comes through any gate or door that opens onto it. Four old worlds were never made by anyone:
**Overworld**, **Hearthvale**, **the Nether** and **the End**. Each but Overworld was held by the will born with it:
the Nether by its fire dragon, Netherius, the End by his brother, Enderius, and the beings of the End, and Hearthvale
by its long-lived people, the Ithari. Overworld has only short lives, humans and beasts, and nothing to hold it, so the
Void wore away its shores, deepest at **the breach** (Matěj, 2026-10-22, the Story Outline's "Decided so far").

The Ithari of Hearthvale saw where that ended, and their guild of makers, the **Gatewrights**, made worlds that could
be held. A Gatewright raises a gate in Overworld, sets a signet (a ring carrying one pattern) into its sealstone and
opens it onto the Void. The signet presses its pattern into the Void like a ring into wax, and a realm takes shape
there, its people included (Matěj's idea; chapter 2 tells it on the seal chamber's murals). The first was the stone
signet, and the realm it made is Undervault. The Gatewrights called their work, and the world it made,
**Realmforge**.

### The Wall, the seals and the road

The five forged realms stand side by side around Overworld and close its whole shore: **the Wall**. Each realm's
first gate was shut forever, so that the realm would set: that gate is its **seal**, with one side in the Lodge's
great hall in Overworld and one in the realm's seal chamber, where the keeper's face shows in the frame. Held realms
don't wear, so the Void meets the Wall, not Overworld. Every realm is one gate from Overworld, and a gate can only be
raised there: the order of the realms comes from the signets and the level caps, never from distance (Matěj,
2026-10-22).

| Lore | What it is in the game |

| A signet is forged around its shard, with the most precious ore or gem of the realm before it and a little of the realm it opens | the next signet is crafted from that drop in the middle, 4 of the previous realm's ore or gem and 4 of the new realm's theme block; the twins' signets take 2 gems, 2 Netherite Scraps and 4 theme blocks (RLM-395, RLM-432) |

### The keepers

A **keeper** is an Ithari bound into a seal, and every sealed realm has one: the Sealbound. A keeper gives his name
to the stone, which is why keepers' names are never spoken ("Our name was given to the stone, as is the custom",
chapter 2). He can't let go except into the hand of a relief, who comes from Skyhearth and takes his place, like a
changing of the guard, and the cracks in his seal are his tiredness made visible. The Wayfarers walk the gates and
check on the keepers (Matěj, 2026-10-22: keepers rotate, so no keeper "has failed"). Undervault's keeper is
**the Stonebound**.

### The Gatewrights and the Ithari

- **The Ithari** are a long-lived people (thousands of years), human in shape but taller, with faintly gold eyes:
  the Gatewrights, the Wayfarers and Skyhearth's people are all Ithari (Matěj, 2026-10-22, RLM-357).
- **The Gatewrights** are the Ithari's guild of makers ("wright" is an old word for a maker). Their works are
  everywhere: ruins, gate frames, vaults, catacombs, statues, murals, and inscriptions in a blocky script that the
  Lodge has slowly learned to read. They left the gate design, the signet craft, the guardians and their trials, the
  Hewn (stone servants), the hall of welcome before Undervault's keeper, and their writings, which are the lore's
  main carrier (WF-7).
- **The founder,** the First Gatewright, opened the first gates himself. Every hall the Gatewrights built has his
  statue, a Gatewright holding a signet up to the light, and the keepers remember his visits: he asked what the Void
  looked like from their seal (chapter 2). His name is **Elenyor**, and in the finale he is **Elenyor, the
  Unforger** (Matěj, RLM-357).
- **The Wayfarers' Lodge** is the Ithari's old order of road-wardens: they walk the gates, check on the keepers and
  escort the reliefs. Its great hall stands in Overworld over the breach, with the five seals' Overworld sides in a
  ring and the stone signet's vault behind them. Since the war its archive and its last Wayfarers live in Skyhearth
  (chapter 1).
- **Skyhearth** is the Gatewrights' council city of white stone with gold between its bricks. It floated in
  Hearthvale's sky; since the war it floats above Overworld and the whole Wall, home to Hearthvale's refugees under
  their king, **Taveth** (Matěj, 2026-10-22). Its platforms are lifts, not gates: they rise to it from any realm
  ([The hub realm: Skyhearth](#the-hub-realm-skyhearth)).

### The war, 700 years ago

Within a few decades, about 700 years ago, three old worlds were lost. The Nether was destroyed, and its gates with
it. Netherius, the Worldburner burned Hearthvale into **Ashfire**, and most of the Ithari who survived fled to
Skyhearth. The End was reshaped into **Unmade Isles**. When the war came, the Wayfarers went up to Skyhearth and left
behind the humans who had worked at the Lodge's hall, who were forced to stay, fight and hide: the illagers
(chapter 1).

The truth is Matěj's decision of 2026-10-22 and stays hidden until the finale: the founder listened to the Void until
it took him over. He took Netherius, drew the Nether's fire out into an army, sent it into Hearthvale, and went to
unmake the End, where he got stuck part-way and cannot leave Unmade Isles. Taveth, Skyhearth's king, is secretly the
last of the Gatewright council; the player learns it on Unmade Isles. How the council caged the founder, and what
Taveth hides, are the Story Outline draft's, waiting for Matěj.

### Why now

Since the war, reliefs have been rare: too few Ithari are left to give a keeper's years. The oldest keepers are past
their strength, and their seals crack, in the realms and in the Lodge's hall. The cracks shake Overworld too, and old
crypts split open (chapter 1); in the realms the tremors wake things the Ithari can't fight, such as the Hewn
(chapter 2). Maren Vell reads it plainly: someone has to walk the road again, clear each realm so that reliefs can
follow, and pass each guardian's trial for the next signet.

The road's first signet, the stone signet, waited in the vault of the Lodge's hall. The Illusioner, the illagers'
chief, stole it to reach the Gatewrights' storehouse, and when he tried to force the seal with it, it broke in his
hands. Every Illusioner carries a piece, the **Stone Signet Shard**; bound with diamonds and stone, it forges the
Signet of the Undervault again (Matěj, 2026-10-24, RLM-395; chapter 1). A lost signet comes from another Illusioner's
shard, or from the Lodge Merchant ([realm portal spec](../design/realm-portal.md#realm-signets)).

## The player

- **Role:** a human newcomer, capable and curious, whom the Lodge calls a **Wayfarer**. Not a chosen one: the seals
  accept anyone who passes their trials, and only a human grows stronger by passing them ("My people pass every
  gate, and the trials stand aside for us, so the road never changes us. You grow with every trial you pass",
  chapter 1). Texts never assume the player's race, class, gender or name ([style guide](style.md#the-player)).
- **The arc,** a prologue and one chapter per main realm (Matěj, 2026-10-22; how the game numbers them is RLM-466):
  1. **Overworld (chapter 1, The Ominous Tale, levels 1–10):** Maren's trial, the open crypts, the illagers, the
     Lodge's hall, the first gate and the Illusioner ([chapter 1](chapters/01-the-ominous-tale.md)).
  2. **Undervault (chapter 2, The Weight of Stone, levels 6–18):** the forge-stock, the Cairnfolk and the Hewn, the
     catacombs, the Stonebound and Astone, the Unbroken ([chapter 2](chapters/02-the-weight-of-stone.md)).
  3. **Skyhearth (from about level 15, a mandatory side quest, M3 or later):** the player rises to Skyhearth with
     the Skyhearth Medallion, meets Maren in person and Taveth, and is sent on to the twins.
  4. **The twins (chapters 3 and 4):** Sunwastes and Everwinter, under one level cap (RLM-367).
  5. **Deepholm, Quagmire and Ashfire (chapters 5 to 7):** each realm's crisis comes from the strain on its seal, and
     Ashfire's guardian is Netherius, the Worldburner.
  6. **Unmade Isles (chapter 8, the last of v1):** the finale.
- **The finale (Matěj, RLM-139, RLM-357):** in the Void Cathedral on Unmade Isles the player faces Elenyor, the
  Unforger, the founder taken by the Void. How it ends, and whether the last seal is forged anew from the main
  path's signets as the [Void realm design](../design/realms.md#the-void-realm) proposes (Matěj decides it in
  RLM-164), wait for Matěj's review of the Story Outline draft. Play goes on after it: the realms, their dungeons and
  later the contracts stay.

### Overworld

The heart of the world and the hub: every gate opens into it, and a gate can only be raised here. It is the humans'
home and has no seal and no signet. The Lodge's great hall stands over the breach, with the five seals' Overworld
sides in a ring, and long-abandoned Wayfarer outposts dot the land. Its danger is the ordinary wild (night monsters,
crypts the cracks broke open) and the **illagers**: the humans the Wayfarers left behind in the war, bitter for 700
years, who now claim the gates and are bad on their own, serving nobody (Matěj, 2026-10-22). Their chief, Illusioner,
the Ominous Chief, stole the stone signet. [Chapter 1](chapters/01-the-ominous-tale.md) tells it. In M1 the Lodge is
a voice in the quest book; the Lodge's hall is a unique structure in M2 (RLM-393), and Maren's Image comes with
Skyhearth's Mirror in M2.

### The stone realm: Undervault

The first realm the Gatewrights forged, stamped by the stone signet: one giant cave of stone, with stone above and
below, its people included. They kept their forge-stock in it for every realm after and buried their dead in it,
and called it their vault. Glowstone glitters in its ceiling, and crystals in the catacombs once steered light from
hall to hall (the light-beam puzzles). The forge-stock is ancient debris, sunk below the walkable floor and fused
there over an age: metal born in fire, carried in from the Nether long before the war, and the last netherite in
Realmforge since the Nether was destroyed. Where the stone signet pressed hardest into the Void, the stone set into
gems, ruby, sapphire and topaz, which the Cairnfolk call the signet's marks.

- **The Cairnfolk** were forged with the realm and awaken with the stone. Their sole purpose is to mine it, as the
  Gatewrights forged them to. They are a sturdy, cheerful mining folk who have never seen the sky (it upsets them),
  and they are amazed that anyone comes from above. Each Cairnfolk house has a **Keykeeper**, a generated Cairnfolk
  with a random name who trades ore for the catacomb key (RLM-448, M4).
- **The Hewn** are the Gatewrights' stone servants, carved to guard their storehouse and their catacombs. They stood
  still for ages, until the tremors from the cracking seal woke them; with nobody left to give orders, they keep
  everyone out. **Hewn Sentinels**, each carved around a gem heart, hold the doors with stone fists, and **Hewn
  Chanters** form stones out of thin air and throw them. In the **Hewn's Mine**, **Hewn Miners** still dig the
  forge-stock and **Hewn Overseers** guard it: anyone who takes the Gatewrights' ore is a thief to them.
- **Halcyar, the Dust-Crowned** and his six **Plinth Knights** stand in a hall of welcome of Skyhearth's white and
  gold stone, built for whoever would one day come for the keeper's trial; nobody came, not in thousands of years.
- **The keeper, the Stonebound,** is bound into the stone seal at the end of the stone arena: terse, formal, speaking
  as "we", and asking for a relief. He gives the means to call the trial to the altar.
- **The guardian, Astone, the Unbroken,** is a colossus of living stone who rises out of the arena: a trial, not a
  villain. She is she/her, and in the story she falls once and for all, into rubble that lies still (Matěj,
  2026-10-27). The Gatewrights sealed inside her the shards of the next two signets, the Sun Signet Shard and the Ice
  Signet Shard; in the rubble lies her heart, the Stone Heart, which opens the way to Skyhearth.
- **Others:** the Golem Works, a works of metal servants whose makers stay unknown in chapter 2, and Rubia, living
  rock of ruby and stone who guards the deepest ruby seams in the Cairnfolk's old hold (optional, RLM-253).
- **Lore carriers:** inscriptions on catacomb sarcophagi (the lore riddles of the realm design), the seal chamber's
  murals of the First Forging, the Stonebound's words, and the Keykeepers' lines. Chapter 2 tells it
  ([chapter 2](chapters/02-the-weight-of-stone.md)).

### The desert realm: Sunwastes

The sun realm, one of the twins: the Gatewrights stamped it and Everwinter as a matched pair, sun and ice. Its
people, the Sun Court, built pyramids and a great sphinx as vaults for their treasures, and buried their own forges
under the sand when the seal closed.

- **The Wellkeepers** live in the big oases and in the few towns not yet empty. They know the dunes, guard the
  water and trade with anyone who respects it.
- **The Gilded** are the Sun Court's servants, who covered themselves in gold to outlast the sealing. They did,
  after a fashion: sand-dry and gold-masked, they still guard the pyramids and raid the oases for what they think is
  theirs.
- **The guardian** is a great sphinx in its own dungeon, which holds one of the two shards the next seal needs.
  Its trial is the realm's riddles as much as its claws.

### The tundra realm: Everwinter

The frost realm, the other twin, stamped to hold something still, and it held: ships froze in the lakes mid-voyage
and a sea-raiding people slept under the ice for an age.

- **The Kettlefolk** live in stilt villages around the hot springs, fishing through the ice. They are warm, nosy
  and generous, and they know which lakes hum at night.
- **The Thawed** are the raiders under the ice, waking as the seal thins. They are not dead, only very late, and
  they want their fortress, their ships and their sea back. Their nordic fortress and their frozen shipwrecks are
  the realm's dungeons.
- **The guardian** is the Ice Queen in the ice castle, who holds the other shard that seal needs ("{name}, the Ice
  Queen"; her name is open, RLM-357). The two shards together forge the Signet of Deepholm.

### The hub realm: Skyhearth

The Gatewrights' council city of white stone with gold between its bricks, which floated in Hearthvale's sky until
the war and now floats above Overworld and the whole Wall, over the Lodge's hall. It has no seal, no guardian and no
trial. Its people are Hearthvale's refugees under their king, **Taveth**, the name the refugees gave him; the Lodge's
archive and its last Wayfarers live there, Maren Vell among them. Unique NPCs live only here (Matěj, 2026-10-15):
Edric, the Explorer, who remembers the green land and the dragon in the fire, Bonnie, the Jeweler, and, once the
player opens the way, Torva Flintmark. Its people know the realms beyond Undervault and send Wayfarers into them from
about level 15, and it is the centre of the game: its stories, quests, trades and friendships run through every
later chapter (Matěj, 2026-10-17). Nothing there can be built or broken.

Skyhearth admits from below only those who carry a guardian's heart: a Wayfarer crafts the Skyhearth Medallion from
the Stone Heart and the three Undervault gems, builds the platform, stands in its middle and rises (chapter 2, M3 or
later; RLM-245, RLM-302). The council hall holds the law of relief, carved into its wall, and a map of the Wall whose
stops light up as the player opens them. Maren's archive holds the founder's old letters ("The Void is not empty. It
is patient."). Its history and people are written with the realm
([realm design](../design/realms.md#the-hub-realm)).

### The water realm: Deepholm

The ocean realm, the fourth of the Forging ("the Deep One", chapter 1): sand hills, coral reefs and the wrecks of
ships on its floor, water reaching nearly to the sky, and nothing above it but an empty surface. A city lies on the
seabed. Its peoples and guardian are written with its chapter
([realm design](../design/realms.md#the-water-realm)).

### The swamp realm: Quagmire

The mire, the last realm of the Forging ("The Mire, which nobody liked", chapter 1): a sunken, steaming realm of
slime of many kinds, in swamps and in banks of slime. Its peoples and guardian are written with its chapter.

### The fire realm: Ashfire

Hearthvale, the Ithari's own old world of green plains, rivers and mountains, until Netherius, the Worldburner burned
it 700 years ago (Matěj, 2026-10-22, RLM-357). Now its plains are black and red stone under burnt trees, with lava
lakes and volcanoes, and it comes deliberately late on the road: what burns here is too strong for a newcomer. It is
not the Nether, and nothing in it is (Matěj, 2026-10-16), though its burner was the Nether's dragon.

- **The Emberwrights** are the Ithari who stayed in burnt Hearthvale, the best crafters on the road; their staying
  still holds the realm.
- **Netherius's army** of Nether beings still roams it; its kinds are written with the chapter.
- **The guardian** is **Netherius, the Worldburner**, a fire dragon who fights on the ground (vision). His story is
  written with the chapter.

### The Void realm: Unmade Isles

The End, half unforged by the founder in the war (Matěj, 2026-10-22): islands drifting over the Void, bound together
with great chains and bridges, with dark stone gates and a cathedral on the farthest island. The Void here is not
dark: it glows violet, under a pale moon, and the isles' purple stone, dark wood and crystals catch that light
(RE-18). Where the Void touches forged stone a green ore grows, malachite, the one thing the Void seems unable to
unmake.

- **The Chainkeepers** are the End's survivors, who chain the drifting isles together and still hold them, each
  keeping one link from a link-house where a chain is anchored. They are solemn, few and very glad to see anyone. They
  say the chains catch whoever falls, and the Void gives them back (the realm design's fall rule).
- **The Unforged** are the beings of the End, half unforged: half-made shapes, faded and wrong in colour and
  material. Where they strike, the Void unmakes a little of what a Wayfarer wears, unless it is malachite. They are
  strange rather than gruesome.
- **Enderius, the Worldender,** Netherius's brother and the End's dragon, still serves the founder there as a boss
  (about level 57, RLM-357).
- **The final boss** is Elenyor, the Unforger, in the Void Cathedral ([The player](#the-player)).
- The [realm design](../design/realms.md#the-void-realm) designs this realm (RLM-95); Matěj's approval of that design
  is RLM-164.

### Further realms

The road can grow beyond v1 with the [candidate realms](../design/realms.md#candidate-realms). The dense forest is
dropped (Matěj, RLM-188); the underwater realm, dropped then too, became Deepholm (Matěj, 2026-10-19, RLM-265), and
the sky islands' celestial palace became the hub, now Skyhearth.

A new realm fits the road when it has: one element (RE-5) and a look of its own (RE-20); a friendly and a hostile
people; a guardian holding the shards of the seals after it; and a reason it exists in the story.

The friendly peoples are the realms' quest-givers, traders and, once villages are reworked, their villagers. Outside
Skyhearth, NPCs are generated with random names and a role, like the Cairnfolk Keykeepers (RLM-448). A faction that
gives repeatable trades (the Cairnfolk taking ore) is also a natural home for the repeatable quests of a chapter.

### Recurring characters

- **Maren Vell**, Ithari archivist of the Wayfarers' Lodge in Skyhearth: the player's mentor and the voice of the
  quest journal. Scholarly, kind, a little dry; she reads Gatewright script better than anyone alive and had never
  seen Undervault ("It's bigger than the books"). She reaches the player as **Maren's Image**, taller than anyone
  the player knows, a little see-through, with a faint gold in her eyes, through **Skyhearth's Mirror**, which a
  Skyhearth raven brings in chapter 1. The player meets her in person in Skyhearth.
- **The Stonebound**, Undervault's keeper: bound into the stone seal, his face in its frame; slow to speak, formal,
  and always "we". Every sealed realm has a keeper like him, so the player meets the same kind of figure before each
  guardian.
- **Taveth**, Skyhearth's king, known by the name the refugees gave him, and secretly the last of the Gatewright
  council (revealed on Unmade Isles, Matěj, 2026-10-22).
- **Torva Flintmark**, the first Cairnfolk to see a sky: blunt and cheerful, she came up to Skyhearth after the
  player opened the way, and sells the late-game dungeon keys there (RLM-181). She is not in Undervault: generated
  Keykeepers carry the keys there (RLM-448).
- **Edric, the Explorer** and **Bonnie, the Jeweler**, of Skyhearth (RLM-244, RLM-248, RLM-357).
- **Illusioner, the Ominous Chief**, chief of the illagers, who stole and broke the stone signet (chapter 1).
- **Elenyor, the Unforger**, the First Gatewright: heard throughout the road in the inscriptions and letters he left,
  seen in his statues, met only at the end.

## What the lore leaves open

The outline forces none of these; each is decided elsewhere, and the lore follows whatever is decided:

- **The rest of the story.** The Realmforge Story Outline doc (draft 8) holds the full story: how the council caged
  the founder, Taveth's secret and why he keeps it, chapters 3 to 8 and the finale, with the ending Matěj likes
  (Maren takes the post). It waits for his review; this outline takes in each part as he approves it.
- **The game's chapter numbers** (RLM-466): the story counts eight chapters, the twins as chapters 3 and 4; the
  design documents and `content/chapters/` count seven, the twins as one.
- **The Lodge's hall** is one unique structure per world in M2, which Matěj builds (RLM-393); a Lodge house in a
  village may still come with the M4 village overhaul (RLM-139).
- **Friendly NPCs in M1.** There are none (Matěj, 2026-10-15), and the M1 versions of chapters 1 and 2 work without
  them: the quest book speaks.
- **Deepholm and Quagmire.** Their lines above give each a reason to exist; their peoples, guardians and history are
  written with their chapters.
- **Boss mechanics, mob and item names** (W3, W5, A1). The outline gives the bosses and peoples an identity; the
  tasks that build them name and shape them, within the [style guide](style.md#names).
- **The Void realm's gameplay** ([realm design](../design/realms.md#the-void-realm), RLM-95, waiting for Matěj in
  RLM-164): the last seal forged from the main-path signets, malachite gear (the lore says the Void cannot unmake
  malachite, which the proposed set bonus follows) and the fall rule. If he changes one, the lines that tell it
  change with it.

## Decisions

Matěj's answers of 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14 (RLM-139), and the decisions since, which this version records:

1. **The outline is approved:** the Gatewrights made the realms against the Void; signets open seals; each guardian
   is a trial that holds the shards of the seals after it; the Wayfarers' Lodge sends the player along the road. The
   [style guide](style.md) is approved too.
2. **The names are approved as proposed:** Realmforge, the Gatewrights, the Chain of Seals, the Wayfarers' Lodge,
   Maren Vell, Torva Flintmark, the Cairnfolk, the Hewn, the Unbroken, the Scribe in the Stone and the other
   peoples in the tables (the Scribe became the Stonebound, and the Unbroken Astone, the Unbroken: item 10).
   - **Realm names:** Undervault, Sunwastes, Rimefields, Forgefire and Brink; Overworld stays Overworld (three were
     renamed on 2026-10-15, item 7). Where a player first meets a realm's name (the guide, the signet tooltip, the
     arrival title), it carries a plain subtitle with its element, such as "Undervault — Stone Realm". The
     [realm design](../design/realms.md) uses these names.
   - **"Maren Vell" stays** (2026-10-14). Said aloud it is "Ma-ren Vell", not Marvel's "Mar-Vell", and "Vell"
     echoes vellum, which suits an archivist.
3. **The finale:** the founder, hollowed out by the Void, is the final antagonist in the Void Cathedral, so the Void
   realm is on the main path (his name and title since 2026-10-22: item 9). Forging the last seal from every
   main-path signet is a gameplay idea for the Void realm design:
   [RLM-95 proposes it](../design/realms.md#the-void-realm), and Matěj decides it in RLM-164. Side realms stay
   optional, so their signets are never part of it.
4. **The level cap lore is used:** texts may say that the seals bind strength and that the bond loosens once every
   trial of a chapter is passed. The rule itself is unchanged.
5. **The Lodge is a voice in the quest book for M1;** a Lodge house in a village may come with the M4 village
   overhaul.
6. **No Czech texts, ever:** the game is English only ([vision](../vision.md)), so the style guide has no rules for
   other languages.
7. **The v1 realm layout** (Matěj, 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-16, RLM-188):
   - **renames:** the Rimefields became **Everwinter**, the Forgefire **Ashfire**, and the Brink **Unmade Isles**;
   - **no article:** a realm name never takes "the": Overworld, Undervault, Sunwastes, Everwinter, Olympus,
     Prestoria, Quagmire, Ashfire, Unmade Isles;
   - **new realms:** Olympus (the hub, from level 30), Prestoria (prehistoric) and Quagmire (swamp); the forest and
     underwater realms are dropped;
   - **chapters:** a realm is a chapter and the twins share one, so chapter 1 is Overworld (levels 1–10), chapter 2
     Undervault (6–18), and so on to chapter 7, Unmade Isles (48–60). M1 ships chapters 1 and 2 (the story's eight
     chapters since 2026-10-22: item 9, RLM-466);
   - **Ashfire is not the Nether** and is not inspired by it (2026-10-16).
8. **Two later renames and the article in sentences:**
   - **Olympus became Asgard** (Matěj, 2026-10-17, RLM-245), whose king is **Odin**. It opens from about level 15,
     through the Asgard Medallion (named on 2026-10-20, RLM-302), and is the centre of the game, with quests into the
     twins and every later realm (now Skyhearth, Taveth and the Skyhearth Medallion: item 9);
   - **Prestoria became Atlantis** (Matěj, 2026-10-19, RLM-265), a water realm in the same chapter 4 band. The
     prehistoric theme left with it, and the underwater realm RLM-188 dropped came back (now Deepholm: item 9);
   - **the article in sentences** (Matěj, 2026-10-19, RLM-265): a realm's name still has no article, but a sentence
     that refers to the realm takes "the" where it reads naturally, as with the Nether ("Enter the Nether"), and
     every realm's entry quest is titled "Enter the {realm}" ([style guide](style.md#text-types)).
9. **The world of the Story Outline** (Matěj, 2026-10-22, RLM-357 and the Story Outline doc's "Decided so far"):
   - **names:** the race of the Gatewrights and the Wayfarers is the **Ithari**; Asgard became **Skyhearth**, Odin
     **Taveth** and Atlantis **Deepholm**, to drop borrowed mythology; the sphinx stays. The final boss is
     **Elenyor, the Unforger**, historically **the First Gatewright**, which replaces "the First Wright"; Ashfire's
     final boss is **Netherius, the Worldburner**, and his brother **Enderius, the Worldender** is a boss on Unmade
     Isles; Everwinter's is "{name}, the Ice Queen"; Ashfire was **Hearthvale** before Netherius burned it; NPCs and
     bosses are written "Name, the Title" (Edric, the Explorer; Bonnie, the Jeweler);
   - **the world:** the Gatewrights stamp a realm by setting its signet into a gate opened onto the Void; the five
     forged realms stand around Overworld; every realm is one gate from Overworld; keepers are relieved like a
     changing of the guard; Hearthvale became Ashfire and the End Unmade Isles; Skyhearth floated above Hearthvale
     and moved over Overworld, and its platforms reach it from any realm; Maren lives in Skyhearth;
   - **the founder's arc:** first good, then taken by the Void; he destroyed the Nether, went to unmake the End and
     is stuck on Unmade Isles. Taveth is the last of the Gatewright council, hidden until Unmade Isles; the illagers
     are bad on their own and serve nobody;
   - **the structure:** a prologue, then one chapter per main realm, eight in all, with Skyhearth as a mandatory side
     quest from level 15; the twins share level cap 30 (RLM-367).
10. **Chapters 1 and 2** (Matěj, 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-27, RLM-351):
    - **approved:** [chapter 1](chapters/01-the-ominous-tale.md) on 2026-10-24 and
      [chapter 2](chapters/02-the-weight-of-stone.md) on 2026-10-27;
    - **renames:** Heaven's Mirror became **Skyhearth's Mirror** (2026-10-25), the Scribe in the Stone **the
      Stonebound** (2026-10-25), and the Unbroken **Astone, the Unbroken** (2026-10-25), who is she/her and falls once
      and for all in the story (2026-10-27);
    - **new characters and places:** Halcyar, the Dust-Crowned and the Plinth Knights (RLM-451), the Hewn Miners and
      Overseers of the Hewn's Mine (RLM-428, RLM-429), and the Cairnfolk Keykeepers (RLM-448);
    - **the Cairnfolk** were forged with the realm: "Their sole purpose is to mine it, as the Gatewrights forged them
      to" (2026-10-27); **Torva Flintmark** lives only in Skyhearth (2026-10-26);
    - **the Nether** is part of the lore: its gates were destroyed with it, and netherite comes from Undervault's
      forge-stock (2026-10-27).
